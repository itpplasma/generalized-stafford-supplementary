Attribution, not used by the proof

Credited in the paper; no Lean declaration formalizes these, and the Lean proof does not depend on them.

  • Stafford 1978b, Thm 5.1 (p. 232): Let R be a simple left Noetherian ring with Kdim R > r and M a torsion left R-module with Kdim M ≤ r. If Kdim M/(Ra + Rb) < r for some a, b ∈ M, then for any d ≠ 0 there is f with Kdim M/R(a + dfb) < r. Its mirror for right modules would give the single-test case of Lemma select (an unaudited reconstruction); the Lean proof does not use it.
  • Stafford 1982, Prop. 1.3 (p. 74): Let R be a simple Noetherian non-Artinian ring and M = ⊕ a_i R a finitely generated Artinian right module, f ≠ 0. Then M = (a_1 + Σ_{i≥2} a_i λ_i f)R for some λ_i; in particular M is cyclic. Gives Theorem A when R/dR has finite length (δ(d) = 0), for two-sided Noetherian R; prescribed-tail form that Lemma select generalizes. Not used by the Lean proof.
  • Robson 1976, p. 152 and proof of Thm 4.3 (pp. 152, 166): For a simple Noetherian ring and the quotient category by modules of Krull dimension < m, the image of a module of Krull dimension m has finite length.
