Challenge transport: the Weyl algebra statements

The definitions of the Weyl part of GeneralizedStaffordChallenge.lean (identical copy Challenge.lean) are restated verbatim below, in the same namespaces and under the same names, so that the solution never imports the challenge. Each challenge theorem <name> is proved here as <name>proof​; GeneralizedStaffordSolution.lean restates it under its challenge name.

The Weyl algebra is Stafford38Challenge.WeylAlg, the presented algebra of the registered challenge. Its proofs are the AlgebraicAnalysis Weyl facts of GeneralizedStafford.Weyl, which use AlgebraicAnalysis' WeylAlg; the two are the same presentation, with the same relation body, so they agree definitionally.

Weyl algebra: two-generated one-sided ideals

The Weyl algebra An​(k) is the presented algebra of the registered Stafford 3.8 challenge. The definitions Stafford.freeWeylRelation, Stafford.FreeWeyl, Stafford38Challenge.PhaseVar, Stafford38Challenge.WeylAlg, Stafford.Reduction.Stafford38, Stafford38Challenge.UniversalStatement and Stafford38.TorsionCyclicity.IsRightTorsion are copied verbatim from stafford38-formal/Stafford38/ChallengeDefinitions.lean; only the imports differ.

The left summand of PhaseVar n indexes xi​, the right summand ∂i​; the relations ιiιj−ιjιi=Jij with Matrix.J = [[0, −1], [1, 0]] give [∂i​,xj​]=δij​, all other generators commuting.

The commutator relations presenting a Weyl-type algebra.

45/-- The commutator relations presenting a Weyl-type algebra. -/
46def freeWeylRelation (omega : Matrix ι ι k)
47 (a b : FreeAlgebra k ι) : Prop :=
48 ∃ i j,
49 a = FreeAlgebra.ι k i * FreeAlgebra.ι k j -
50 FreeAlgebra.ι k j * FreeAlgebra.ι k i ∧
51 b = algebraMap k (FreeAlgebra k ι) (omega i j)

53/-- The free algebra modulo the commutator relations encoded by `omega`. -/

55 (omega : Matrix ι ι k) := RingQuot (freeWeylRelation omega)

94 [AddCommGroup M] [Module Aᵐᵒᵖ M] : Prop :=

**Affine form (Theorem A for An​(k)).** For every nonzero d, every nonzero a and every B in An​(k) there are H,U,V with 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV.

118/-- **Affine form (Theorem A for `Aₙ(k)`).** For every nonzero `d`, every
119nonzero `a` and every `B` in `Aₙ(k)` there are `H, U, V` with
120`1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`. -/
121def AffineFormStatement : Prop :=
122 ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [CharZero k] (n : ℕ) (d a B : WeylAlg k n),
123 d ≠ 0 → a ≠ 0 → ∃ H U V : WeylAlg k n, 1 = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V

125/-- **Fixed-ideal form (Theorem A' for `Aₙ(k)`).** For every nonzero right

Prescribed first generator, right ideals (C.2). If a is a nonzero element of a right ideal I of An​(k), then for every y∈I and every nonzero t there is h∈I with I=aAn​+(y+ht)An​. (Since An​(k) is a domain, any nonzero a∈I may serve as the first generator.)

179/-- **Prescribed first generator, right ideals (C.2).** If `a` is a nonzero
180element of a right ideal `I` of `Aₙ(k)`, then for every `y ∈ I` and every
181nonzero `t` there is `h ∈ I` with `I = a Aₙ + (y + h t) Aₙ`. (Since `Aₙ(k)` is a
182domain, any nonzero `a ∈ I` may serve as the first generator.) -/
183def PrescribedTwoGeneratedRightStatement : Prop :=
184 ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [CharZero k] (n : ℕ)
185 (I : Submodule (WeylAlg k n)ᵐᵒᵖ (WeylAlg k n)) (a : WeylAlg k n),
186 a ∈ I → a ≠ 0 → ∀ y ∈ I, ∀ t : WeylAlg k n, t ≠ 0 →
187 ∃ h ∈ I, I = Submodule.span (WeylAlg k n)ᵐᵒᵖ {a} ⊔
188 Submodule.span (WeylAlg k n)ᵐᵒᵖ {y + h * t}
