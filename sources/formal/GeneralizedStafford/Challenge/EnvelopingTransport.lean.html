Challenge transport: simple quotients of enveloping algebras

The definitions of the enveloping-algebra section of GeneralizedStaffordChallenge.lean (identical copy Challenge.lean) are restated verbatim below, in the same namespace and under the same names. The conclusion predicates (FixedAnchorProperty, ...) and the bridge lemmas are those of GeneralizedStafford/Challenge/FilteredTransport.lean, which this file imports; they are the same declarations used for the filtered rings.

Each compared enveloping theorem <name> of the challenge is proved here as <name>proof​; GeneralizedStaffordSolution.lean restates it under its challenge name. envelopingSide applies the library theorem GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses and translates its deviation statements into the challenge form.

Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, hypotheses. For a finite-dimensional Lie algebra g over an infinite field k and a simple quotient R of U(g), R and Rop are Noetherian, have infinite centre and have Krull dimension at most dimg.

31/-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, hypotheses.** For a finite-dimensional
32Lie algebra `𝔤` over an infinite field `k` and a simple quotient `R` of `U(𝔤)`,
33`R` and `Rᵐᵒᵖ` are Noetherian, have infinite centre and have Krull dimension at most
34`dim 𝔤`. -/
35def EnvelopingHypothesesStatement : Prop :=
36 ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [Infinite k] (L : Type u) [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
37 [FiniteDimensional k L] (R : Type u) [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
38 (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →
39 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
40 DevDimLE R R (Module.finrank k L)) ∧
41 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
42 DevDimLE Rᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L))

Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A. Under the hypotheses of EnvelopingHypothesesStatement, Theorem A holds for R (right form 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV) and for Rop (left form 1=Ud+Vd(B+aH) of R).

44/-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A.** Under the hypotheses of
45`EnvelopingHypothesesStatement`, Theorem A holds for `R` (right form
46`1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`) and for `Rᵐᵒᵖ` (left form `1 = U d + V d (B + a H)` of `R`). -/
47def EnvelopingTheoremAStatement : Prop :=
48 ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [Infinite k] (L : Type u) [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
49 [FiniteDimensional k L] (R : Type u) [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
50 (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →
51 FixedAnchorProperty R ∧ FixedAnchorProperty Rᵐᵒᵖ
52

Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A'. Under the hypotheses of EnvelopingHypothesesStatement, Theorem A' holds for R and for Rop.

53/-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A'.** Under the hypotheses of
54`EnvelopingHypothesesStatement`, Theorem A' holds for `R` and for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
55def EnvelopingFixedIdealStatement : Prop :=

Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Corollary C.1. Under the hypotheses of EnvelopingHypothesesStatement, the prescribed-generator and stable forms of C.1 hold for R and for Rop.

61/-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Corollary C.1.** Under the hypotheses of
62`EnvelopingHypothesesStatement`, the prescribed-generator and stable forms of C.1 hold for
63`R` and for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
64def EnvelopingPrescribedCyclicStatement : Prop :=

69 (PrescribedCyclicProperty.{u, v} Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ StablyCyclicProperty.{u, v} Rᵐᵒᵖ)

Bridge: (N), (Z), Kdim≤dimg and (H_omega) on both sides

80/-! ## Bridge: (N), (Z), `Kdim ≤ dim 𝔤` and (H_omega) on both sides -/

82

Both sides of a simple quotient of U(g) satisfy (N), (Z), the deviation bound Kdim≤dimg and (H_omega), from GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses.

82/-- Both sides of a simple quotient of `U(𝔤)` satisfy (N), (Z), the deviation bound
83`Kdim ≤ dim 𝔤` and (H_omega), from `GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses`. -/
84theorem envelopingSide {k L R : Type u} [Field k] [Infinite k] [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
85 [FiniteDimensional k L] [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
86 (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (hf : Function.Surjective f) :
87 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
88 DevDimLE R R (Module.finrank k L) ∧ HOmega R) ∧
89 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
90 DevDimLE Rᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L) ∧ HOmega Rᵐᵒᵖ) := by
91 obtain ⟨⟨hN1, hZ1, hD1, hO1⟩, ⟨hN2, hZ2, hD2, hO2⟩⟩ :=
92 GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses f hf
93 exact ⟨⟨hN1, hZ1, (devDimLE_iff _).mpr hD1, hOmega_iff.mpr hO1⟩,
94 ⟨hN2, hZ2, (devDimLE_iff _).mpr hD2, hOmega_iff.mpr hO2⟩⟩
