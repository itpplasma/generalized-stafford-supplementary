8 (identical copy `Challenge.lean`) are restated verbatim below, in the same namespace and
9 under the same names. The conclusion predicates (`FixedAnchorProperty`, ...) and the

27 /-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, hypotheses.** For a finite-dimensional
28 Lie algebra `𝔤` over an infinite field `k` and a simple quotient `R` of `U(𝔤)`,
29 `R` and `Rᵐᵒᵖ` are Noetherian, have infinite centre and have Krull dimension at most
30 `dim 𝔤`. -/
31 def EnvelopingHypothesesStatement : Prop :=
32   ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [Infinite k] (L : Type u) [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
33     [FiniteDimensional k L] (R : Type u) [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
34     (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →
35     (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
36         DevDimLE R R (Module.finrank k L)) ∧
37       (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
38         DevDimLE Rᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L))
39 
40 /-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A.** Under the hypotheses of
41 `EnvelopingHypothesesStatement`, Theorem A holds for `R` (right form
42 `1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`) and for `Rᵐᵒᵖ` (left form `1 = U d + V d (B + a H)` of `R`). -/
43 def EnvelopingTheoremAStatement : Prop :=
44   ∀ (k : Type u) [Field k] [Infinite k] (L : Type u) [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
45     [FiniteDimensional k L] (R : Type u) [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
46     (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →
47     FixedAnchorProperty R ∧ FixedAnchorProperty Rᵐᵒᵖ
48 
49 /-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Theorem A'.** Under the hypotheses of
50 `EnvelopingHypothesesStatement`, Theorem A' holds for `R` and for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
51 def EnvelopingFixedIdealStatement : Prop :=

54     (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →
55     FixedIdealProperty R ∧ FixedIdealProperty Rᵐᵒᵖ
56 
57 /-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Corollary B.** Under the hypotheses of

63     (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R), Function.Surjective f →

66 /-- **Simple quotients of enveloping algebras, Corollary C.1.** Under the hypotheses of

93 /-! ## Bridge: (N), (Z), `Kdim ≤ dim 𝔤` and (H_omega) on both sides -/
94 
95 /-- Both sides of a simple quotient of `U(𝔤)` satisfy (N), (Z), the deviation bound
96 `Kdim ≤ dim 𝔤` and (H_omega), from `GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses`. -/
97 theorem envelopingSide {k L R : Type u} [Field k] [Infinite k] [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra k L]
98     [FiniteDimensional k L] [Ring R] [Algebra k R] [IsSimpleRing R]
99     (f : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (hf : Function.Surjective f) :
100     (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
101         DevDimLE R R (Module.finrank k L) ∧ HOmega R) ∧
102       (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
103         DevDimLE Rᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L) ∧ HOmega Rᵐᵒᵖ) := by
104   obtain ⟨⟨hN1, hZ1, hD1, hO1⟩, ⟨hN2, hZ2, hD2, hO2⟩⟩ :=
105     GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses f hf
106   exact ⟨⟨hN1, hZ1, (devDimLE_iff _).mpr hD1, hOmega_iff.mpr hO1⟩,
107     ⟨hN2, hZ2, (devDimLE_iff _).mpr hD2, hOmega_iff.mpr hO2⟩⟩
108
