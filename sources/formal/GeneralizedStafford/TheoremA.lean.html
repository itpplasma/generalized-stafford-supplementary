Theorem A' (fixed ideal) and Theorem A (fixed anchor)

Assume (S) R simple, (N) right Noetherian, and (Z) k an infinite central field. Zero divisors are allowed: the anchor d and the regular element of I are regular, while the tails t and a need only be nonzero.

  • Theorem A' (fixed_ideal) is P(N+1) of the two-call descent with the empty family of tests: KdimR/(I+fR)<0, i.e. I+fR=R.
  • Theorem A (fixed_anchor) is Theorem A' for I=dR, x=Bd and t=ad (nonzero because d is regular): dR+(Bd+Had)R=R reads 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV.

Theorem A' (fixed ideal). Let I be a right ideal containing a regular element with Kdim(R/I)≤N finite. For every x∈R and every t=0 there is H∈R with I+(x+Ht)R=R.

31/-- **Theorem A' (fixed ideal).** Let `I` be a right ideal containing a
32regular element with `Kdim(R/I) ≤ N` finite. For every `x ∈ R`
33and every `t ≠ 0` there is `H ∈ R` with `I + (x + H t)R = R`. -/
34theorem fixed_ideal {I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R} (hreg : ∃ s ∈ I, s ∈ R⁰)
35 {N : ℕ} (hfin : Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ N (R ⧸ I)) (x : R) {t : R} (ht : t ≠ 0) :
36 ∃ H : R, I ⊔ principalRightIdeal (x + H * t) = ⊤ := by
37 obtain ⟨H, hsmall, -⟩ := idealBound_zero (k := k) hreg hfin x t ht
38 (fun i : PEmpty.{u + 1} => nomatch i)
39 exact ⟨H, eq_top_of_small_zero hsmall⟩

Theorem A (fixed anchor). Let d be regular with δ(d)=Kdim(R/dR)≤N finite. For every a=0 (possibly a zero-divisor) and every B there are H,U,V with 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV.

42/-- **Theorem A (fixed anchor).** Let `d` be regular with
43`δ(d) = Kdim(R/dR) ≤ N` finite. For every `a ≠ 0` (possibly a zero-divisor)
44and every `B` there are `H, U, V` with `1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`. -/
45theorem fixed_anchor {d : R} (hd : d ∈ R⁰) {N : ℕ}
46 (hfin : Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ N (R ⧸ principalRightIdeal d)) {a : R} (ha : a ≠ 0) (B : R) :
47 ∃ H U V : R, 1 = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V := by
48 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := fixed_ideal (k := k) (I := principalRightIdeal d)
49 ⟨d, ⟨1, mul_one d⟩, hd⟩ hfin (B * d) (t := a * d) (fun h0 => ha (hd.2 a h0))
50 have h1 : (1 : R) ∈ principalRightIdeal d ⊔ principalRightIdeal (B * d + H * (a * d)) := by
51 rw [hH]; exact Submodule.mem_top
52 obtain ⟨y, ⟨U, rfl⟩, z, ⟨V, rfl⟩, he⟩ := Submodule.mem_sup.mp h1
53 refine ⟨H, U, V, ?_⟩
54 rw [← he]
55 change d * U + (B * d + H * (a * d)) * V = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V
56 simp only [add_mul, mul_assoc]
