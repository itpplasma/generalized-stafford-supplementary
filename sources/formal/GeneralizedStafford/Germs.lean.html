Germs and source selection

The Gabriel quotient Fe​ and its germs are represented concretely by the germ calculus of AlgebraicAnalysis.Module.Germ: a germ of level e of a module X is an element of SectionReX=HomRopeRX, i.e. a partial map from a submodule of R with small cokernel, modulo maps that agree on a dense submodule; Hom.Epic w is "w is epic modulo Kdim<e".

  • Central scalars. A central λ∈k acts on sections of a critical module through the centre of its Schur division ring (section_central_scalar).
  • Unit germs. qJ​r is epic iff KdimR/(J+rR)<e (smul_mkQ_epic_iff).
  • Epic criterion, pencils and pencil stability. An epic germ v stays epic along v+uλ for all but finitely many λ (germ_pencil_finite).
  • Escape and maximal rank. These are AlgebraicAnalysis.Goldie.IsGoldieTorsion.exists_smul_mul_not_mem and IsGoldieTorsion.exists_injective_right_mul; together with the pencil lemma they give finite affine source selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules (exists_affine_source_of_goldie).
  • Source selection. A test (GermTest) is (e,V,q,z,c) with q an epic germ and c regular; f satisfies it if z+qfc is epic. Every finite family of tests is satisfied by some f=x+Ht (GermTest.exists_affine_satisfied), and a satisfied test stays satisfied along f+λg for almost all λ (GermTest.pencil_finite).

10# Germs and source selection
11
12The Gabriel quotient `F_e` and its germs are represented concretely by the
13germ calculus of `AlgebraicAnalysis.Module.Germ`: a germ of level `e` of a
14module `X` is an element of `Section R e X = Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ e R X`, i.e. a partial
15map from a submodule of `R` with small cokernel, modulo maps that agree on a
16dense submodule; `Hom.Epic w` is "`w` is epic modulo `Kdim < e`".
17
18* **Central scalars.** A central `λ ∈ k` acts on sections of a critical
19 module through the centre of its Schur division ring
20 (`section_central_scalar`).
21* **Unit germs.** `q_J r` is epic iff `Kdim R/(J + rR) < e`
22 (`smul_mkQ_epic_iff`).
23* **Epic criterion, pencils and pencil stability.** An epic
24 germ `v` stays epic along `v + u λ` for all but finitely many `λ`
25 (`germ_pencil_finite`).
26* **Escape and maximal rank.** These are
27 `AlgebraicAnalysis.Goldie.IsGoldieTorsion.exists_smul_mul_not_mem` and
28 `IsGoldieTorsion.exists_injective_right_mul`; together with the pencil lemma
29 they give finite affine source selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules
30 (`exists_affine_source_of_goldie`).
31* **Source selection.** A *test* (`GermTest`) is
32 `(e, V, q, z, c)` with `q` an epic germ and `c` regular; `f` satisfies it if
33 `z + q f c` is epic. Every finite family of tests is satisfied by some
34 `f = x + H t` (`GermTest.exists_affine_satisfied`), and a satisfied test stays
35 satisfied along `f + λ g` for almost all `λ` (`GermTest.pencil_finite`).
36-/

Right multiplication of a section by a central scalar λ is the left action of the Schur endomorphism φλ​ , also for germs defined only on a dense submodule.

57/-- Right multiplication of a section by a central scalar `λ` is the left
58action of the Schur endomorphism `φ_λ` , also for germs defined only on a dense submodule. -/
59theorem section_central_scalar (a : Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) (w : Section R e C) :
60 a.val • w = End.centralMap (R := Rᵐᵒᵖ) (e := e) (C := C) a • w := by
61 have he : Hom.reducedMap (e := e) (End.centralScalar (C := C) a) =
62 End.centralScalar (C := C ⧸ smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e C) a := by
63 apply LinearMap.ext
64 intro x
65 obtain ⟨x, rfl⟩ := Submodule.mkQ_surjective (smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e C) x
66 rfl
67 have hs : Hom.reducedMap (e := e) (Section.leftMul (unop a.val)) =
68 End.centralScalar (C := R ⧸ smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e R) a := by
69 apply LinearMap.ext
70 intro x
71 obtain ⟨x, rfl⟩ := Submodule.mkQ_surjective (smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e R) x
72 change (smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e R).mkQ (unop a.val * x) =
73 (smallRadical Rᵐᵒᵖ e R).mkQ (x * unop a.val)
74 congr 1
75 exact congrArg unop (Subring.mem_center_iff.mp a.property (op x))
76 change PartialHom.comp w (PartialHom.ofLinearMap _) =
77 PartialHom.comp (PartialHom.ofLinearMap _) w
78 rw [he, hs]
79 induction w using Quotient.inductionOn with | _ w =>
80 apply Quotient.sound
81 apply Representative.rel_of_eq
82 intro x hx hy
83 have hm := w.val.toFun.map_smul a.val (⟨x, w.pullback_le _ hy⟩ : w.val.domain)
84 exact hm

Pencil stability. If v is an epic germ of a module M with KdimM≤e and u is any germ, then v+uλ is epic for all but finitely many λ∈k. Proof: by the epic criterion it suffices to test the finitely many critical top factors C of a critical filtration; on each, h↦h(v+uλ)=Pv​h+φλ​Pu​h is a central pencil with Pv​ injective, so it is injective for almost all λ.

129/-- **Pencil stability.** If `v` is an epic germ of a module `M` with
130`Kdim M ≤ e` and `u` is any germ, then `v + u λ` is epic for all but finitely
131many `λ ∈ k`. Proof: by the epic criterion it suffices to test the
132finitely many critical top factors `C` of a critical filtration; on each,
133`h ↦ h(v + u λ) = P_v h + φ_λ P_u h` is a central pencil with `P_v` injective,
134so it is injective for almost all `λ`. -/
135theorem germ_pencil_finite (hM : Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ e M)
136 (F : Module.CriticalFiltration Rᵐᵒᵖ e M) (v u : Section R e M) (hv : Hom.Epic v) :
137 {c : k | ¬ Hom.Epic (v + op (algebraMap k R c) • u)}.Finite := by
138 classical
139 have hfin : (⋃ i : F.TopIndex,
140 {c : k | ¬ Function.Injective (fun h : Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ e M (F.TopFactor i) =>
141 Hom.comp h (v + op (algebraMap k R c) • u))}).Finite := by
142 refine Set.finite_iUnion fun i => ?_
143 let := End.divisionRing (F.topFactor_critical i)
144 let := Hom.finite_of_devLE hM (F.topFactor_critical i)
145 let κ : k →+* End Rᵐᵒᵖ e (F.TopFactor i) :=
146 End.centralMap.comp (oppositeCentralField (R := R) k)
147 have hκ : ∀ c x, κ c * x = x * κ c := fun c x =>
148 (End.centralMap_commute (oppositeCentralField (R := R) k c) x).eq
149 refine (centralPencil_injective_finite κ hκ (Hom.precompLinear v)
150 (Hom.precompLinear u) (fun h h' he => hv.right_cancel h h' he)).subset ?_
151 intro c hc hinj
152 apply hc
153 have heq : (fun h : Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ e M (F.TopFactor i) =>
154 Hom.comp h (v + op (algebraMap k R c) • u)) =
155 centralPencil κ hκ (Hom.precompLinear v) (Hom.precompLinear u) c := by
156 funext h
157 rw [Hom.comp_add]
158 change Hom.comp h v + Hom.comp h (Hom.comp u (Hom.ofLinearMap
159 (Section.leftMul (algebraMap k R c)))) = Hom.comp h v + κ c • Hom.comp h u
160 rw [← Hom.comp_assoc]
161 exact congrArg (Hom.comp h v + ·)
162 (section_central_scalar (oppositeCentralField (R := R) k c) (Hom.comp h u))
163 rw [heq]
164 exact hinj
165 refine hfin.subset fun c hc => ?_
166 by_contra hn
167 apply hc
168 refine (Hom.epic_iff_injective_comp_topFactors hM F _).mpr fun i => ?_
169 by_contra hi
170 exact hn (Set.mem_iUnion.mpr ⟨i, hi⟩)
171
172end Stability

Finite affine selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules, direction form (Steps 1 and 2 below). Let Pi​ be injective maps from finite-dimensional spaces into Goldie-torsion bimodules Wi​, Zi​ arbitrary, a=0 and bi​ regular. There is a direction H such that d↦Zi​d+Pi​(d)(B+σ(c)Ha)bi​ is injective for all i and all but finitely many central scalars c. Step 1 combines maximal rank for each i by ordinary pencils (AlgebraicAnalysis exists_simultaneously_injective); Step 2 is a reversed pencil (AlgebraicAnalysis reversed_centralPencil_injective_finite).

187/-- Finite affine selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules, direction form
188(Steps 1 and 2 below). Let `Pᵢ` be injective maps from finite-dimensional
189spaces into Goldie-torsion bimodules `Wᵢ`, `Zᵢ` arbitrary, `a ≠ 0` and `bᵢ`
190regular. There is a direction `H` such that `d ↦ Zᵢ d + Pᵢ(d) (B + σ(c) H a) bᵢ`
191is injective for all `i` and all but finitely many central scalars `c`.
192Step 1 combines maximal rank for each `i` by ordinary pencils
193(AlgebraicAnalysis `exists_simultaneously_injective`); Step 2 is a reversed
194pencil (AlgebraicAnalysis `reversed_centralPencil_injective_finite`). -/
195theorem exists_affine_direction_of_goldie
196 (hW : ∀ i, IsGoldieTorsion R (Wᵢ i))
197 (σ : k →+* R) (κ : ∀ i, k →+* Eᵢ i)
198 (hκ : ∀ i c a, κ i c * a = a * κ i c)
199 (hscalar : ∀ i c (w : Wᵢ i), op (σ c) • w = κ i c • w)
200 (P Z : ∀ i, Dᵢ i →ₗ[Eᵢ i] Wᵢ i) (hP : ∀ i, Function.Injective (P i))
201 (s : Finset ι) (b : ι → R) (hb : ∀ i ∈ s, b i ∈ R⁰)
202 {a : R} (ha : a ≠ 0) (B : R) :
203 ∃ H : R, {c : k | ∃ i ∈ s, ¬ Function.Injective
204 (fun d => Z i d + op ((B + σ c * H * a) * b i) • P i d)}.Finite := by
205 classical
206 let F (i : ι) (r : R) : Dᵢ i →ₗ[Eᵢ i] Wᵢ i :=
207 (rightMulLinear (E := Eᵢ i) (r * (a * b i))).comp (P i)
208 have hF (i : ι) (r t : R) (c : k) (d : Dᵢ i) :
209 F i (r + σ c * t) d = F i r d + κ i c • F i t d := by
210 change op ((r + σ c * t) * (a * b i)) • P i d =
211 op (r * (a * b i)) • P i d + κ i c • (op (t * (a * b i)) • P i d)
212 rw [add_mul, op_add, add_smul, mul_assoc]
213 congr 1
214 rw [op_mul, mul_smul, hscalar, smul_comm]
215 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := exists_simultaneously_injective σ κ hκ F hF s (fun i hi =>
216 (hW i).exists_injective_right_mul σ (κ i) (hκ i) (hscalar i)
217 (P i) (hP i) (fun h0 => ha ((hb i hi).2 a h0)))
218 let A (i : ι) : Dᵢ i →ₗ[Eᵢ i] Wᵢ i :=
219 Z i + (rightMulLinear (E := Eᵢ i) (B * b i)).comp (P i)
220 have hfinite : (⋃ i ∈ s,
221 {c | ¬ Function.Injective (centralPencil (κ i) (hκ i) (A i) (F i H) c)}).Finite :=
222 s.finite_toSet.biUnion fun i hi =>
223 reversed_centralPencil_injective_finite (κ i) (hκ i) _ _ (hH i hi)
224 refine ⟨H, hfinite.subset ?_⟩
225 rintro c ⟨i, hi, hbad⟩
226 refine Set.mem_iUnion.mpr ⟨i, Set.mem_iUnion.mpr ⟨hi, ?_⟩⟩
227 have heq : (fun d => Z i d + op ((B + σ c * H * a) * b i) • P i d) =
228 centralPencil (κ i) (hκ i) (A i) (F i H) c := by
229 funext d
230 change Z i d + op ((B + σ c * H * a) * b i) • P i d =
231 (Z i d + op (B * b i) • P i d) + κ i c • (op (H * (a * b i)) • P i d)
232 have hscaled : op (σ c * (H * (a * b i))) • P i d =
233 κ i c • (op (H * (a * b i)) • P i d) := by
234 rw [op_mul, mul_smul, hscalar, smul_comm]
235 rw [add_mul, op_add, add_smul, mul_assoc, mul_assoc, hscaled]
236 abel
237 change ¬ Function.Injective (centralPencil (κ i) (hκ i) (A i) (F i H) c)
238 rwa [← heq]

Finite affine selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules: some H makes every d↦Zi​d+Pi​(d)(B+Ha)bi​ injective (take H := σ(c) H₀ for a good central c).

240/-- Finite affine selection in Goldie-torsion bimodules: some `H` makes every
241`d ↦ Zᵢ d + Pᵢ(d) (B + H a) bᵢ` injective (take `H := σ(c) H₀` for a good
242central `c`). -/
243theorem exists_affine_source_of_goldie
244 (hW : ∀ i, IsGoldieTorsion R (Wᵢ i))
245 (σ : k →+* R) (κ : ∀ i, k →+* Eᵢ i)
246 (hκ : ∀ i c a, κ i c * a = a * κ i c)
247 (hscalar : ∀ i c (w : Wᵢ i), op (σ c) • w = κ i c • w)
248 (P Z : ∀ i, Dᵢ i →ₗ[Eᵢ i] Wᵢ i) (hP : ∀ i, Function.Injective (P i))
249 (s : Finset ι) (b : ι → R) (hb : ∀ i ∈ s, b i ∈ R⁰)
250 {a : R} (ha : a ≠ 0) (B : R) :
251 ∃ H : R, ∀ i ∈ s, Function.Injective
252 (fun d => Z i d + op ((B + H * a) * b i) • P i d) := by
253 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := exists_affine_direction_of_goldie hW σ κ hκ hscalar P Z hP s b hb ha B
254 obtain ⟨c, hc⟩ := hH.exists_notMem
255 refine ⟨σ c * H, ?_⟩
256 intro i hi
257 by_contra hbad
258 exact hc ⟨i, hi, hbad⟩

260end FiniteSelection

Source selection for finitely many critical germ tests: given epic germs qi​, offsets zi​, regular tails bi​, a=0 and B, some H makes every h↦h(zi​+qi​(B+Ha)bi​) injective on the Schur space of the Goldie-torsion critical module Ci​.

311/-- Source selection for finitely many critical germ tests: given epic germs
312`qᵢ`, offsets `zᵢ`, regular tails `bᵢ`, `a ≠ 0` and `B`, some `H` makes every
313`h ↦ h (zᵢ + qᵢ (B + H a) bᵢ)` injective on the Schur space of the
314Goldie-torsion critical module `Cᵢ`. -/
315theorem exists_affine_germ_tests
316 {ι : Type*} [Fintype ι] (e : ι → ℕ) (M C : ι → Type u)
317 [∀ i, AddCommGroup (M i)] [∀ i, Module Rᵐᵒᵖ (M i)]
318 [∀ i, IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ (M i)]
319 [∀ i, AddCommGroup (C i)] [∀ i, Module Rᵐᵒᵖ (C i)]
320 [∀ i, IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ (C i)]
321 (hM : ∀ i, Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (M i))
322 (hC : ∀ i, Module.Critical Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (C i))
323 (ht : ∀ i, IsGoldieTorsion R (C i))
324 (q z : ∀ i, Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) R (M i)) (hq : ∀ i, Hom.Epic (q i))
325 (b : ι → R) (hb : ∀ i, b i ∈ R⁰) {a : R} (ha : a ≠ 0) (B : R) :
326 ∃ H : R, ∀ i, Function.Injective
327 (fun h : Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (M i) (C i) =>
328 Hom.comp h (z i + op ((B + H * a) * b i) • q i)) := by
329 classical
330 let : ∀ i, DivisionRing (End Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (C i)) := fun i => End.divisionRing (hC i)
331 let : ∀ i, Module.Finite (End Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (C i))
332 (Hom Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (M i) (C i)) := fun i => Hom.finite_of_devLE (hM i) (hC i)
333 let κ (i : ι) : k →+* End Rᵐᵒᵖ (e i) (C i) :=
334 End.centralMap.comp (oppositeCentralField (R := R) k)
335 let P (i : ι) := Hom.precompLinear (C := C i) (q i)
336 let Z (i : ι) := Hom.precompLinear (C := C i) (z i)
337 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := exists_affine_source_of_goldie
338 (fun i => isGoldieTorsion_section (hC i) (ht i))
