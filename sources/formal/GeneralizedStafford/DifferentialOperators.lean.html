Rings of differential operators Dk​(A)

Let k be a field of characteristic zero and A a smooth domain of finite type over k. The ring D=Dk​(A) of finite-order k-linear differential operators on A satisfies (S), (N), (Z) and (H_omega) on both sides, by AlgebraicAnalysis (SmoothOperators): D is simple and a domain, it is right and left Noetherian, its right and left deviations are at most 2r for r generators of A, and its centre is infinite. The conclusions of Theorem A, Theorem A', Corollary B and Corollaries C.1 and C.2 then follow for D and for Dop from the abstract theorems of GeneralizedStafford.Challenge.FilteredTransport.

This file proves:

  • GeneralizedStafford.DiffOp.conclusions_right, conclusions_left: all conclusions for D (right ideals and modules) and for Dop (left ideals and modules).
  • affineForm_right, affineForm_left: Theorem A, right form 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV and left form 1=Ud+Vd(B+aH), for nonzero d and a, as statements about elements of Module.End k A lying in Dk​(A).
  • globalStafford: 1=dR+FdS for nonzero d.
  • twoGenerated_left_ideal, prescribedCyclicLeft: the left-ideal and left-module forms, with the same proofs transported through the opposite ring.

13Let `k` be a field of characteristic zero and `A` a smooth domain of finite type over `k`.
14The ring `D = D_k(A)` of finite-order `k`-linear differential operators on `A` satisfies (S),

17`2r` for `r` generators of `A`, and its centre is infinite. The conclusions of Theorem A,
18Theorem A', Corollary B and Corollaries C.1 and C.2 then follow for `D` and for `Dᵐᵒᵖ` from
19the abstract theorems of `GeneralizedStafford.Challenge.FilteredTransport`.

147/-- **Theorem A, right form.** For differential operators `d, a, B` on `A` with `d ≠ 0` and
