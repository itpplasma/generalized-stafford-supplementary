Simple quotients of enveloping algebras

Let g be a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over an infinite field k, and let R be a simple k-algebra with a surjective k-algebra map π:U(g)→R (so R≅U(g)/J for a two-sided ideal J). Then R and Rop satisfy (N), (Z) and Kdim≤dimg, and hence Theorem A, Theorem A', Corollary B and Corollary C hold for R on both sides.

Proof idea. The PBW symbol presentation of AlgebraicAnalysis (pbwSymbolPresentation) presents the filtration of R induced by the generators of g by the polynomial ring in dimg variables, so R is Noetherian on both sides with deviation at most dimg. The opposite statements are the same statements for left ideals, transported by isNoetherian_right_op_iff and devLE_right_op_iff. The conclusions then follow from the abstract theorems of GeneralizedStafford.Filtered, using Module.DevLE.omega.

  • GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_hypotheses: (N), (Z) and Kdim≤dimg on both sides.
  • GeneralizedStafford.enveloping_theoremA and the following theorems: the conclusions for each side.

**Enveloping algebras: (N), (Z) and Kdim≤dimg on both sides.** Let k be an infinite field, g a finite-dimensional Lie algebra and R a simple quotient of U(g), given by a surjective algebra map π. Then R is Noetherian with infinite centre and right deviation at most dimg and at most ω; the same holds for Rop.

42/-- **Enveloping algebras: (N), (Z) and `Kdim ≤ dim 𝔤` on both sides.** Let `k` be an
43infinite field, `𝔤` a finite-dimensional Lie algebra and `R` a simple quotient of `U(𝔤)`,
44given by a surjective algebra map `π`. Then `R` is Noetherian with infinite centre and
45right deviation at most `dim 𝔤` and at most `ω`; the same holds for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
46theorem enveloping_hypotheses [Infinite k] [IsSimpleRing R]
47 (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (hπ : Function.Surjective π) :
48 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
49 Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L) R ∧
50 Module.DevLEOmega Rᵐᵒᵖ R) ∧
51 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
52 Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L) Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧
53 Module.DevLEOmega Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ) := by
54 have hD := devLE_right_of_surjective π hπ
55 have hDL : Module.DevLE R (Module.finrank k L) R := devLE_left_of_surjective π hπ
56 have hop : IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ := (isNoetherian_right_op_iff R).mpr (isNoetherianRing_of_surjective π hπ)
57 have hopD : Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ (Module.finrank k L) Rᵐᵒᵖ := (devLE_right_op_iff R _).mpr hDL
58 exact ⟨⟨isNoetherian_right_of_surjective π hπ, infinite_subring_center k R, hD, hD.omega⟩,
59 ⟨hop, infinite_subring_center k Rᵐᵒᵖ, hopD, hopD.omega⟩⟩

Theorem A, both sides (enveloping algebras). For every regular d, every nonzero a and every B there are H,U,V with 1=dU+(B+Ha)dV, in R and in Rop (the left form 1=Ud+Vd(B+aH) of R).

68/-- **Theorem A, both sides (enveloping algebras).** For every regular `d`, every nonzero
69`a` and every `B` there are `H, U, V` with `1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`, in `R` and in `Rᵐᵒᵖ`
70(the left form `1 = U d + V d (B + a H)` of `R`). -/
71theorem enveloping_theoremA (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
72 (hπ : Function.Surjective π) :
73 (∀ d a B : R, d ∈ R⁰ → a ≠ 0 → ∃ H U V : R, 1 = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V) ∧
74 (∀ d a B : Rᵐᵒᵖ, d ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰ → a ≠ 0 →
75 ∃ H U V : Rᵐᵒᵖ, 1 = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V) := by
76 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := enveloping_hypotheses π hπ
77 have := hN
78 have := hN'
79 exact ⟨fun d a B hd ha => fixed_anchor_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO hd ha B,
80 fun d a B hd ha => fixed_anchor_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO' hd ha B⟩

Theorem A', both sides (enveloping algebras). For every right ideal I of R containing a regular element, every x and every nonzero t there is H with I+(x+Ht)R=R; the same holds for Rop.

82/-- **Theorem A', both sides (enveloping algebras).** For every right ideal `I` of `R`
83containing a regular element, every `x` and every nonzero `t` there is `H` with
84`I + (x + H t) R = R`; the same holds for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
85theorem enveloping_fixedIdeal (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
86 (hπ : Function.Surjective π) :
87 (∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R, (∃ s ∈ I, s ∈ R⁰) → ∀ x t : R, t ≠ 0 →
88 ∃ H : R, I ⊔ principalRightIdeal (x + H * t) = ⊤) ∧
89 (∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ, (∃ s ∈ I, s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰) → ∀ x t : Rᵐᵒᵖ, t ≠ 0 →
90 ∃ H : Rᵐᵒᵖ, I ⊔ principalRightIdeal (x + H * t) = ⊤) := by
91 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := enveloping_hypotheses π hπ
92 have := hN
93 have := hN'
94 exact ⟨fun I hreg x t ht => by
95 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := fixed_ideal_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO hreg x ht
96 exact ⟨H, hH⟩,
97 fun I hreg x t ht => by
98 obtain ⟨H, hH⟩ := fixed_ideal_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO' hreg x ht
99 exact ⟨H, hH⟩⟩

Corollary C.1, both sides (enveloping algebras). Every finitely generated Goldie-torsion right module M over R (and over Rop) satisfies: for every x∈M and every nonzero t there is h∈M with M=(x+ht)R. Also, for Goldie-torsion elements c,u of M and every nonzero t there is H with cR+uR=(c+uHt)R.

101/-- **Corollary C.1, both sides (enveloping algebras).** Every finitely generated
102Goldie-torsion right module `M` over `R` (and over `Rᵐᵒᵖ`) satisfies: for every `x ∈ M` and
103every nonzero `t` there is `h ∈ M` with `M = (x + h t) R`. Also, for Goldie-torsion elements
104`c, u` of `M` and every nonzero `t` there is `H` with `cR + uR = (c + u H t) R`. -/
105theorem enveloping_prescribedCyclic (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
106 (hπ : Function.Surjective π) :
107 ((∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖ M] [Module.Finite Rᵐᵒᵖ M],
108 IsGoldieTorsion R M → ∀ (x : M) (t : R), t ≠ 0 →
109 ∃ h : M, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {x + op t • h} = ⊤) ∧
110 (∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖ M] (c u : M),
111 (∃ s ∈ R⁰, op s • c = 0) → (∃ s ∈ R⁰, op s • u = 0) → ∀ t : R, t ≠ 0 →
112 ∃ H : R, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {c, u} = Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {c + op (H * t) • u})) ∧
113 ((∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M] [Module.Finite Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M],
114 IsGoldieTorsion Rᵐᵒᵖ M → ∀ (x : M) (t : Rᵐᵒᵖ), t ≠ 0 →
115 ∃ h : M, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {x + op t • h} = ⊤) ∧
116 (∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M] (c u : M),
117 (∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, op s • c = 0) → (∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, op s • u = 0) → ∀ t : Rᵐᵒᵖ, t ≠ 0 →
118 ∃ H : Rᵐᵒᵖ, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {c, u} =
119 Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {c + op (H * t) • u})) := by
120 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := enveloping_hypotheses π hπ
121 have := hN
122 have := hN'
123 refine ⟨⟨fun M _ _ _ htor x t ht => prescribed_cyclic_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO htor x ht,
124 fun M _ _ c u hc hu t ht => ?_⟩,
125 ⟨fun M _ _ _ htor x t ht => prescribed_cyclic_of_devLE_omega (k := k) hO' htor x ht,
126 fun M _ _ c u hc hu t ht => ?_⟩⟩
127 · obtain ⟨N, hN⟩ := devLE_span_of_torsion (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO) hu
128 exact span_pair_eq_span_affine (k := k) c u hc hu ⟨N, hN⟩ ht
129 · obtain ⟨N, hN⟩ := devLE_span_of_torsion (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO') hu
130 exact span_pair_eq_span_affine (k := k) c u hc hu ⟨N, hN⟩ ht

Corollary C.2, both sides (enveloping algebras). Let I be a right ideal of R and a∈I with aQ=IQ, that is every y∈I has a regular s with ys∈aR. Then for every y∈I and nonzero t there is h∈I with I=aR+(y+ht)R, and every right ideal of R is generated by two elements; the same holds for Rop.

132/-- **Corollary C.2, both sides (enveloping algebras).** Let `I` be a right ideal of `R`
133and `a ∈ I` with `aQ = IQ`, that is every `y ∈ I` has a regular `s` with `y s ∈ aR`. Then
134for every `y ∈ I` and nonzero `t` there is `h ∈ I` with `I = aR + (y + h t) R`, and every
135right ideal of `R` is generated by two elements; the same holds for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
136theorem enveloping_twoGenerated (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
137 (hπ : Function.Surjective π) :
138 ((∀ (I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R) (a : R), a ∈ I →
139 (∀ y ∈ I, ∃ s ∈ R⁰, y * s ∈ principalRightIdeal a) →
140 ∀ y ∈ I, ∀ t : R, t ≠ 0 →
141 ∃ h ∈ I, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal (y + h * t)) ∧
142 ∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R, ∃ a b : R, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal b) ∧
143 ((∀ (I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ) (a : Rᵐᵒᵖ), a ∈ I →
144 (∀ y ∈ I, ∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, y * s ∈ principalRightIdeal a) →
145 ∀ y ∈ I, ∀ t : Rᵐᵒᵖ, t ≠ 0 →
146 ∃ h ∈ I, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal (y + h * t)) ∧
147 ∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ, ∃ a b : Rᵐᵒᵖ,
148 I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal b) := by
149 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := enveloping_hypotheses π hπ
150 have := hN
151 have := hN'
152 exact ⟨⟨fun I a haI hIa y hyI t ht => by
153 obtain ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩ := two_generated_prescribed (k := k)
154 (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO) haI hIa hyI ht
155 exact ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩,
156 fun I => two_generated (k := k) (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO) I⟩,
157 ⟨fun I a haI hIa y hyI t ht => by
158 obtain ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩ := two_generated_prescribed (k := k)
159 (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO') haI hIa hyI ht
160 exact ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩,
161 fun I => two_generated (k := k) (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO') I⟩⟩
