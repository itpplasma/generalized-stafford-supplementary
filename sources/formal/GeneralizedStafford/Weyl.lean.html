The Weyl algebra An​(k)

The hypotheses (S), (N), (Z) and (H_omega) of the generalized Stafford theorems hold for the Weyl algebra An​(k) over a field k of characteristic zero, by AlgebraicAnalysis (Weyl.Simplicity, Weyl.BernsteinSymbol, Weyl.Transposition). This file collects these facts and the consequences for An​(k):

  • unconditionally, every right ideal and every left ideal of An​(k) is two-generated (Corollary C.2), with the left case transferred from the right case by the transposition anti-automorphism;
  • the Weyl algebra is a domain (AlgebraicAnalysis Weyl.isDomain), so every nonzero element is regular; this is the input of the regular-element consequences below (Theorem A, Theorem A', Corollary C.1 and the prescribed-generator form of Corollary C.2).

59/-- The centre of `A_n(k)` contains `k`, which is infinite (Z). -/
60theorem infinite_center (k : Type u) [Field k] [CharZero k] (n : ℕ) :
61 Infinite (Subring.center (WeylAlg k n)) := by
62 have := isSimpleRing k n
63 have hinj : Function.Injective (algebraMap k (WeylAlg k n)) :=
64 (algebraMap k (WeylAlg k n)).injective
65 let f : k → Subring.center (WeylAlg k n) := fun c =>
66 ⟨algebraMap k (WeylAlg k n) c,
67 Subring.mem_center_iff.mpr fun z => (Algebra.commutes c z).symm⟩
68 exact Infinite.of_injective f fun a b hab => hinj (congrArg Subtype.val hab)

Corollary C.2 for a domain: a nonzero element a of a right ideal I is the first generator of a two-generated presentation.

109/-- Corollary C.2 for a domain: a nonzero element `a` of a
110right ideal `I` is the first generator of a two-generated presentation. -/
111theorem prescribedTwoGenerated_of_domain [IsSimpleRing A] [IsNoetherian Aᵐᵒᵖ A]
112 [Infinite (Subring.center A)] [IsDomain A] (hO : HOmega A)
113 (I : Submodule Aᵐᵒᵖ A) (a : A) (haI : a ∈ I) (ha : a ≠ 0) (y : A) (hy : y ∈ I)
114 (t : A) (ht : t ≠ 0) :
115 ∃ h ∈ I, I = Submodule.span Aᵐᵒᵖ {a} ⊔ Submodule.span Aᵐᵒᵖ {y + h * t} := by
116 refine corollaryC2_prescribed_proof A (hAll_of_omega hO)
117 I a haI ?_ y hy t ht
118 intro z _
119 by_cases hz : z = 0
120 · exact ⟨1, isRegular_of_ne_zero one_ne_zero, by simp [hz]⟩
121 obtain ⟨r, s, hrs, _⟩ :=
122 AlgebraicAnalysis.OreRightIntersection.rightOreCondition_of_isNoetherian ha hz
123 have hs : s ≠ 0 := by rintro rfl; simp_all
124 refine ⟨s, isRegular_of_ne_zero hs, ?_⟩
125 rw [← hrs, Submodule.mem_span_singleton]
126 exact ⟨op r, by simp⟩
