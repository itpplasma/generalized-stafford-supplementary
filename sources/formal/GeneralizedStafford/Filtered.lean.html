47/-- **Right side of a symbol.** Let `S` identify `gr R` with a commutative
48Noetherian graded algebra `G` of Krull dimension at most `m`. Then `R` is right
49Noetherian, and the right ideals of `R` have deviation at most `m` and at most

**Filtered rings: (N), (Z) and Kdim≤m on both sides.** Let R be a simple k-algebra, k an infinite field, with a symbol S onto a commutative Noetherian graded algebra of Krull dimension at most m. Then R is right Noetherian with infinite centre and right deviation at most m and at most ω; the same holds for Rop, using the opposite filtration and the opposite symbol.

57/-- **Filtered rings: (N), (Z) and `Kdim ≤ m` on both sides.** Let `R` be a
58simple `k`-algebra, `k` an infinite field, with a symbol `S` onto a commutative
59Noetherian graded algebra of Krull dimension at most `m`. Then `R` is right
60Noetherian with infinite centre and right deviation at most `m` and at most
61`ω`; the same holds for `Rᵐᵒᵖ`, using the opposite filtration and the opposite
62symbol. -/
63theorem filtered_hypotheses [Infinite k] [IsSimpleRing R] [IsNoetherianRing G]
64 [GradedAlgebra 𝒜] {φ : RingFiltration k R} (S : φ.GradedSymbol 𝒜) {m : ℕ}
65 (hm : ringKrullDim G ≤ m) :
66 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R ∧ Infinite (Subring.center R) ∧
67 Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖ m R ∧ Module.DevLEOmega Rᵐᵒᵖ R) ∧
68 (IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Infinite (Subring.center Rᵐᵒᵖ) ∧
69 Module.DevLE Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ m Rᵐᵒᵖ ∧ Module.DevLEOmega Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ) :=
70 ⟨⟨(right_side S hm).1, infinite_subring_center k R, (right_side S hm).2⟩,
71 ⟨(right_side S.opposite hm).1, infinite_subring_center k Rᵐᵒᵖ,
72 (right_side S.opposite hm).2⟩⟩
73
74end Hypotheses

93/-- **Theorem A, both sides.** Let `R` be as in `filtered_hypotheses`. For every
94regular `d`, every nonzero `a` and every `B` there are `H, U, V` with
95`1 = d U + (B + H a) d V`, in `R` and in `Rᵐᵒᵖ` (the left form
96`1 = U d + V d (B + a H)` of `R`). -/

101 ∃ H U V : Rᵐᵒᵖ, 1 = d * U + (B + H * a) * d * V) := by
102 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := filtered_hypotheses S hm

Corollary C.1, both sides. Every finitely generated Goldie-torsion right module M over R (and over Rop) satisfies: for every x∈M and every nonzero t there is h∈M with M=(x+ht)R. Also, for Goldie-torsion elements c,u of M and every nonzero t there is H with cR+uR=(c+uHt)R.

127/-- **Corollary C.1, both sides.** Every finitely generated Goldie-torsion right
128module `M` over `R` (and over `Rᵐᵒᵖ`) satisfies: for every `x ∈ M` and every
129nonzero `t` there is `h ∈ M` with `M = (x + h t) R`. Also, for Goldie-torsion
130elements `c, u` of `M` and every nonzero `t` there is `H` with
131`cR + uR = (c + u H t) R`. -/
132theorem filtered_prescribedCyclic [IsNoetherianRing G] [GradedAlgebra 𝒜] {φ : RingFiltration k R}
133 (S : φ.GradedSymbol 𝒜) {m : ℕ} (hm : ringKrullDim G ≤ m) :
134 ((∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖ M] [Module.Finite Rᵐᵒᵖ M],
135 IsGoldieTorsion R M → ∀ (x : M) (t : R), t ≠ 0 →
136 ∃ h : M, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {x + op t • h} = ⊤) ∧
137 (∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖ M] (c u : M),
138 (∃ s ∈ R⁰, op s • c = 0) → (∃ s ∈ R⁰, op s • u = 0) → ∀ t : R, t ≠ 0 →
139 ∃ H : R, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {c, u} = Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ {c + op (H * t) • u})) ∧
140 ((∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M] [Module.Finite Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M],
141 IsGoldieTorsion Rᵐᵒᵖ M → ∀ (x : M) (t : Rᵐᵒᵖ), t ≠ 0 →
142 ∃ h : M, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {x + op t • h} = ⊤) ∧
143 (∀ (M : Type v) [AddCommGroup M] [Module Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ M] (c u : M),
144 (∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, op s • c = 0) → (∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, op s • u = 0) → ∀ t : Rᵐᵒᵖ, t ≠ 0 →
145 ∃ H : Rᵐᵒᵖ, Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {c, u} =
146 Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ {c + op (H * t) • u})) := by

149 have := hN'

Corollary C.2, both sides. Let I be a right ideal of R and a∈I with aQ=IQ, that is every y∈I has a regular s with ys∈aR. Then for every y∈I and nonzero t there is h∈I with I=aR+(y+ht)R, and every right ideal of R is generated by two elements; the same holds for Rop.

159/-- **Corollary C.2, both sides.** Let `I` be a right ideal of `R` and `a ∈ I`
160with `aQ = IQ`, that is every `y ∈ I` has a regular `s` with `y s ∈ aR`. Then for
161every `y ∈ I` and nonzero `t` there is `h ∈ I` with `I = aR + (y + h t) R`, and
162every right ideal of `R` is generated by two elements; the same holds for
163`Rᵐᵒᵖ`. -/
164theorem filtered_twoGenerated [IsNoetherianRing G] [GradedAlgebra 𝒜] {φ : RingFiltration k R}
165 (S : φ.GradedSymbol 𝒜) {m : ℕ} (hm : ringKrullDim G ≤ m) :
166 ((∀ (I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R) (a : R), a ∈ I →
167 (∀ y ∈ I, ∃ s ∈ R⁰, y * s ∈ principalRightIdeal a) →
168 ∀ y ∈ I, ∀ t : R, t ≠ 0 →
169 ∃ h ∈ I, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal (y + h * t)) ∧
170 ∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R, ∃ a b : R, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal b) ∧
171 ((∀ (I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ) (a : Rᵐᵒᵖ), a ∈ I →
172 (∀ y ∈ I, ∃ s ∈ Rᵐᵒᵖ⁰, y * s ∈ principalRightIdeal a) →
173 ∀ y ∈ I, ∀ t : Rᵐᵒᵖ, t ≠ 0 →
174 ∃ h ∈ I, I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal (y + h * t)) ∧
175 ∀ I : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖᵐᵒᵖ Rᵐᵒᵖ, ∃ a b : Rᵐᵒᵖ,
176 I = principalRightIdeal a ⊔ principalRightIdeal b) := by
177 obtain ⟨⟨hN, -, -, hO⟩, ⟨hN', -, -, hO'⟩⟩ := filtered_hypotheses S hm
178 have := hN
179 have := hN'
180 exact ⟨⟨fun I a haI hIa y hyI t ht => by
181 obtain ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩ := two_generated_prescribed (k := k)
182 (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO) haI hIa hyI ht
183 exact ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩,
184 fun I => two_generated (k := k) (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO) I⟩,
185 ⟨fun I a haI hIa y hyI t ht => by
186 obtain ⟨h, hhI, hI⟩ := two_generated_prescribed (k := k)
187 (principalQuotientsFinite_of_devLE_omega hO') haI hIa hyI ht
