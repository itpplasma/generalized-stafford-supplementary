Polynomial presentations of a commutative associated graded ring

Let φ be an ascending filtration of a k-algebra D whose associated graded module is commutative (RingFiltration.GrCommutative: xy−yx has degree below i+j for x∈Fi, y∈Fj). Suppose a commutative k-algebra A maps onto F0 by ι:A→a​[k]D, and finitely many elements y1​,…,yr​ of F1 generate the filtration in degree one: F(n+1)⊆Fn+Fny1​+⋯+Fnyr​. Then the polynomial ring G = A[X₁, …, X_r], graded by total degree, maps onto grD by aXα↦[ι(a)yα]. This is a surjective symbol presentation (RingFiltration.SymbolPresentation), so D is right and left Noetherian when A is Noetherian, and the ideal lattices of D have deviation at most dimA+r when A has finite Krull dimension.

The monomial yα is the product of the yi​ in the order of the list (Finsupp.toMultiset α).toList. The order does not matter modulo lower degree, by commutativity of grD (list_prod_sub_mem_below_of_perm).

This is the generic layer for rings of differential operators Dk​(A) (with yi​ coordinate derivations), and for enveloping-type algebras (with A=k). Injectivity of the presentation is neither claimed nor needed.

Main definitions and results

  • RingFiltration.GrCommutative: the associated graded module is commutative.
  • RingFiltration.list_prod_sub_mem_below_of_perm: in a filtration with commutative associated graded, permuting a product changes it only in lower degree.
  • RingFiltration.polynomialGrading: total-degree grading of A[X_σ] by k-submodules, with its GradedAlgebra instance.
  • RingFiltration.PolynomialGenerators: the hypotheses above.
  • RingFiltration.PolynomialGenerators.presentation: the surjective symbol presentation A[X_σ] ↠ gr D.
  • RingFiltration.PolynomialGenerators.adjoin_eq_top: D is generated by ι(A) and the yi​.
  • PolynomialGenerators.isNoetherian_right, isNoetherianRing, devLE_right, devLE_left, devLEOmega_right, devLEOmega_left.

References

10# Polynomial presentations of a commutative associated graded ring
11
12Let `φ` be an ascending filtration of a `k`-algebra `D` whose associated graded
13module is commutative (`RingFiltration.GrCommutative`: `x y - y x` has degree
14below `i + j` for `x ∈ F i`, `y ∈ F j`). Suppose a commutative `k`-algebra `A`
15maps onto `F 0` by `ι : A →ₐ[k] D`, and finitely many elements `y₁, …, y_r` of
16`F 1` generate the filtration in degree one:
17`F (n + 1) ⊆ F n + F n y₁ + ⋯ + F n y_r`. Then the polynomial ring
18`G = A[X₁, …, X_r]`, graded by total degree, maps onto `gr D` by
19`a X^α ↦ [ι(a) y^α]`. This is a surjective symbol presentation
20(`RingFiltration.SymbolPresentation`), so `D` is right and left Noetherian
21when `A` is Noetherian, and the ideal lattices of `D` have deviation at most
22`dim A + r` when `A` has finite Krull dimension.
23
24The monomial `y^α` is the product of the `y_i` in the order of the list
25`(Finsupp.toMultiset α).toList`. The order does not matter modulo lower
26degree, by commutativity of `gr D` (`list_prod_sub_mem_below_of_perm`).
27
28This is the generic layer for rings of differential operators `D_k(A)` (with
29`y_i` coordinate derivations), and for enveloping-type algebras (with `A = k`).
30Injectivity of the presentation is neither claimed nor needed.

64/-- The associated graded module of `φ` is commutative: for `x ∈ F i` and
65`y ∈ F j`, the commutator `x y - y x` lies below degree `i + j`. -/

67 ∀ ⦃i j : ℕ⦄ ⦃x y : D⦄, x ∈ φ.F i → y ∈ φ.F j → x * y - y * x ∈ φ.below (i + j)

Products commute modulo lower degree. If the associated graded module is commutative, two products of the same factors fi∈F(di) in permuted orders differ by an element below the total degree.

93/-- **Products commute modulo lower degree.** If the associated graded module
94is commutative, two products of the same factors `f i ∈ F (d i)` in permuted
95orders differ by an element below the total degree. -/
96theorem list_prod_sub_mem_below_of_perm (hφ : φ.GrCommutative) {ι : Type*} (f : ι → D)
97 (d : ι → ℕ) (hf : ∀ i, f i ∈ φ.F (d i)) {l l' : List ι} (h : l.Perm l') :
98 (l.map f).prod - (l'.map f).prod ∈ φ.below (l.map d).sum := by
99 induction h with
100 | nil => simp
101 | cons a _ ih =>
102 simp only [List.map_cons, List.prod_cons, List.sum_cons]
103 rw [← mul_sub]
104 exact φ.mul_mem_below_right (hf a) ih
105 | swap a b l =>
106 simp only [List.map_cons, List.prod_cons, List.sum_cons]
107 rw [← mul_assoc, ← mul_assoc, ← sub_mul, ← add_assoc]
108 exact φ.mul_mem_below_left (hφ (hf b) (hf a)) (φ.list_prod_mem f d hf l)
109 | trans h₁ _ ih₁ ih₂ =>
110 rw [(h₁.map d).sum_eq] at ih₁ ⊢
111 simpa using add_mem ih₁ ih₂

197 have hperm := φ.list_prod_sub_mem_below_of_perm hφ y (fun _ => 1) hy
