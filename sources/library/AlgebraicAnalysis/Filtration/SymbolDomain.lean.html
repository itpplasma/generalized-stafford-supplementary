Filtered rings whose associated graded ring is a domain

Let φ be an ascending, exhaustive, submultiplicative filtration of the R-algebra A, and let S : φ.GradedSymbol 𝒜 identify grA with a commutative graded algebra G=⨁An. If G has no zero divisors, then neither has A; if moreover G is nontrivial and 1∈A0, then A is a domain (the standard argument for filtered rings, cf. [McConnell–Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, §1.6]).

Proof

A nonzero a∈A has an order i=φ.ordera, the least i with a∈Fi. Its symbol in degree i is nonzero, since the kernel of the degree-i symbol is F(i−1) (grAt_order_ne_zero). For nonzero a, b of orders i, j the symbol of ab in degree i+j is the product of the two nonzero symbols, which is nonzero in G, so ab=0.

8# Filtered rings whose associated graded ring is a domain
9
10Let `φ` be an ascending, exhaustive, submultiplicative filtration of the
11`R`-algebra `A`, and let `S : φ.GradedSymbol 𝒜` identify `gr A` with a
12commutative graded algebra `G = ⨁ 𝒜 n`. If `G` has no zero divisors, then
13neither has `A`; if moreover `G` is nontrivial and `1 ∈ 𝒜 0`, then `A` is a
14domain (the standard argument for filtered rings, cf.
15[McConnell–Robson, *Noncommutative Noetherian Rings*, §1.6]).

17## Proof
18
19A nonzero `a ∈ A` has an order `i = φ.order a`, the least `i` with
20`a ∈ F i`. Its symbol in degree `i` is nonzero, since the kernel of the
21degree-`i` symbol is `F (i - 1)` (`grAt_order_ne_zero`). For nonzero `a`, `b`
22of orders `i`, `j` the symbol of `a b` in degree `i + j` is the product of
23the two nonzero symbols, which is nonzero in `G`, so `a b ≠ 0`.

The symbol of a nonzero element in the degree of its order is nonzero.

35/-- The symbol of a nonzero element in the degree of its order is nonzero. -/
36theorem grAt_order_ne_zero {a : A} (ha : a ≠ 0) : S.grAt (φ.order ha) a ≠ 0 := by
37 rw [Ne, S.grAt_eq_zero_iff (φ.mem_order ha)]
38 intro h
39 rcases hi : φ.order ha with _ | i
40 · rw [hi, below_zero, Submodule.mem_bot] at h
41 exact ha h
42 · rw [hi, below_succ] at h
43 have := φ.order_le ha h
44 omega

A filtered ring whose associated graded ring has no zero divisors has no zero divisors.

47/-- **A filtered ring whose associated graded ring has no zero divisors has
48no zero divisors.** -/
49theorem noZeroDivisors [NoZeroDivisors G] : NoZeroDivisors A where
50 eq_zero_or_eq_zero_of_mul_eq_zero {a b} hab := by
51 by_contra h
52 push Not at h
53 obtain ⟨ha, hb⟩ := h
54 have hmul := S.grAt_mul (φ.mem_order ha) (φ.mem_order hb)
55 have h0 : S.grAt (φ.order ha + φ.order hb) (0 : A) = 0 := by
56 rw [S.grAt_of_mem (zero_mem _)]
57 exact congrArg Subtype.val (map_zero (S.gr _))
58 rw [hab, h0] at hmul
59 exact mul_ne_zero (S.grAt_order_ne_zero ha) (S.grAt_order_ne_zero hb) hmul.symm
60
61include S in

A filtered ring is nontrivial when its associated graded ring is nontrivial with 1 of degree 0: an element of F0 with symbol 1 is nonzero.

62/-- A filtered ring is nontrivial when its associated graded ring is
63nontrivial with `1` of degree `0`: an element of `F 0` with symbol `1` is
64nonzero. -/
65theorem nontrivial [Nontrivial G] (h1 : (1 : G) ∈ 𝒜 0) : Nontrivial A := by
66 obtain ⟨u, hu, hsymb⟩ := S.exists_grAt_eq h1
67 refine ⟨⟨u, 0, fun hu0 => one_ne_zero (α := G) ?_⟩⟩
68 rw [← hsymb, S.grAt_eq_zero_iff hu, below_zero, Submodule.mem_bot]
69 exact hu0
70
71include S in

**A filtered ring whose associated graded ring is a domain, with 1 of degree 0, is a domain.**

72/-- **A filtered ring whose associated graded ring is a domain, with `1` of

74theorem isDomain [IsDomain G] (h1 : (1 : G) ∈ 𝒜 0) : IsDomain A :=
75 haveI := S.noZeroDivisors
76 haveI := S.nontrivial h1
77 NoZeroDivisors.to_isDomain A
