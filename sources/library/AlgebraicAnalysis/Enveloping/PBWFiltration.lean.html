PBW filtration of a quotient of a universal enveloping algebra

Let L be a Lie algebra over a field k, and let π:U(L)→a​[k]R be a surjective algebra map onto a k-algebra R (this covers U(L) itself and every quotient U(L)/J). Let genx=π(ιx) be the image of x∈L.

The PBW filtration of R is Fn​=spanx1​⋯xj​:j≤n,xi​∈L, the span of products of at most n Lie elements. It is an ascending filtration in the sense of RingFiltration.

The only commutation fact used is the Lie bracket identity genx∗geny−geny∗genx=gen[x,y], which lies in F1​. Hence a product of degree-one generators changes, under a permutation of its factors, only by an element of lower filtration (wordProd_sub_mem_pbwPiece). No Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem is used: only this spanning direction is needed.

Main definitions and results

  • genπx, wordProdπw: the image of x∈L and the ordered product of a word.
  • pbwPieceπn: the span of products of at most n Lie elements, that is Fn​.
  • pbwFiltrationπhπ: the PBW filtration of R as a RingFiltration k R.
  • wordProd_sub_mem_pbwPiece: permuting a word changes its product only in lower filtration.

The symbol presentation by the polynomial ring and the Krull dimension consequences are in AlgebraicAnalysis.Enveloping.Presentation.

The n-th PBW piece Fn​: the k-span of products of at most n Lie elements.

57/-- The `n`-th PBW piece `Fₙ`: the `k`-span of products of at most `n` Lie elements. -/
58def pbwPiece (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (n : ℕ) : Submodule k R :=
59 Submodule.span k {r | ∃ w : List L, w.length ≤ n ∧ wordProd π w = r}
60
61variable (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
62
63theorem wordProd_nil : wordProd π ([] : List L) = 1 := by
64 simp [wordProd]
65
66theorem wordProd_cons (x : L) (w : List L) :
67 wordProd π (x :: w) = gen π x * wordProd π w := by
68 simp [wordProd]
69
70theorem wordProd_append (u v : List L) :
71 wordProd π (u ++ v) = wordProd π u * wordProd π v := by
72 simp [wordProd]
73
74theorem wordProd_singleton (x : L) : wordProd π [x] = gen π x := by
75 simp [wordProd]
76
77theorem wordProd_mem_pbwPiece {w : List L} {n : ℕ} (hw : w.length ≤ n) :
78 wordProd π w ∈ pbwPiece π n :=
79 Submodule.subset_span ⟨w, hw, rfl⟩
80
81theorem pbwPiece_mono {a b : ℕ} (h : a ≤ b) : pbwPiece π a ≤ pbwPiece π b :=
82 Submodule.span_mono fun _ ⟨w, hw, hr⟩ => ⟨w, hw.trans h, hr⟩
83
84theorem one_mem_pbwPiece (n : ℕ) : (1 : R) ∈ pbwPiece π n := by
85 simpa [wordProd_nil] using wordProd_mem_pbwPiece π (w := []) (n := n) (Nat.zero_le _)
86
87theorem gen_mem_pbwPiece_one (x : L) : gen π x ∈ pbwPiece π 1 := by
88 simpa [wordProd_singleton] using wordProd_mem_pbwPiece π (w := [x]) (n := 1) le_rfl
89
90theorem mul_mem_pbwPiece {i j : ℕ} {a b : R} (ha : a ∈ pbwPiece π i)
91 (hb : b ∈ pbwPiece π j) : a * b ∈ pbwPiece π (i + j) := by
92 have hle : pbwPiece π i * pbwPiece π j ≤ pbwPiece π (i + j) := by
93 rw [pbwPiece, pbwPiece, Submodule.span_mul_span]
94 refine Submodule.span_le.mpr ?_
95 rintro _ ⟨a, ⟨u, hu, rfl⟩, b, ⟨v, hv, rfl⟩, rfl⟩
96 exact Submodule.subset_span ⟨u ++ v, by simp only [List.length_append]; omega,
97 wordProd_append π u v⟩
98 exact hle (Submodule.mul_mem_mul ha hb)

100/-- The PBW filtration of `R` is submultiplicative. -/
101theorem pbwPiece_mul_le (i j : ℕ) : pbwPiece π i * pbwPiece π j ≤ pbwPiece π (i + j) :=
102 Submodule.mul_le.mpr fun _ ha _ hb => mul_mem_pbwPiece π ha hb

The PBW pieces exhaust R when π is surjective: every element of U(L) is a sum of products of Lie generators, by induction on the tensor algebra.

104/-- The PBW pieces exhaust `R` when `π` is surjective: every element of `U(L)` is a sum of
105products of Lie generators, by induction on the tensor algebra. -/
106theorem iSup_pbwPiece_eq_top (hπ : Function.Surjective π) : ⨆ n, pbwPiece π n = ⊤ := by
107 rw [eq_top_iff]
108 intro r _
109 obtain ⟨u, rfl⟩ := hπ r
110 obtain ⟨t, rfl⟩ := Quotient.exists_rep u
111 have key : ∀ t : TensorAlgebra k L, ∃ n, π (mkAlgHom k L t) ∈ pbwPiece π n := by
112 intro t
113 induction t using TensorAlgebra.induction with
114 | algebraMap c =>
115 refine ⟨0, ?_⟩
116 have : π (mkAlgHom k L (algebraMap k (TensorAlgebra k L) c)) = algebraMap k R c := by
117 simp
118 rw [this, Algebra.algebraMap_eq_smul_one]
119 exact Submodule.smul_mem _ c (one_mem_pbwPiece π 0)
120 | ι x =>
121 refine ⟨1, ?_⟩
122 exact gen_mem_pbwPiece_one π x
123 | mul a b ha hb =>
124 obtain ⟨n, hn⟩ := ha
125 obtain ⟨m, hm⟩ := hb
126 refine ⟨n + m, ?_⟩
127 simp only [map_mul]
128 exact mul_mem_pbwPiece π hn hm
129 | add a b ha hb =>
130 obtain ⟨n, hn⟩ := ha
131 obtain ⟨m, hm⟩ := hb
132 refine ⟨max n m, ?_⟩
133 simp only [map_add]
134 exact Submodule.add_mem _ (pbwPiece_mono π (le_max_left n m) hn)
135 (pbwPiece_mono π (le_max_right n m) hm)
136 obtain ⟨n, hn⟩ := key t
137 exact Submodule.mem_iSup_of_mem n hn

The PBW filtration. For a surjective algebra map π:U(L)→R, the pieces Fn​=spanproductsofatmostnLie elements form a RingFiltration k R.

146/-- **The PBW filtration.** For a surjective algebra map `π : U(L) → R`, the pieces
147`Fₙ = span {products of at most n Lie elements}` form a `RingFiltration k R`. -/
148noncomputable def pbwFiltration (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
149 (hπ : Function.Surjective π) : RingFiltration k R where
150 F := pbwPiece π
151 mono := fun _ _ h => pbwPiece_mono π h
152 one_mem := one_mem_pbwPiece π 0
153 mul_le := pbwPiece_mul_le π
154 exhaustive := iSup_pbwPiece_eq_top π hπ
155
156theorem pbwFiltration_F (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (hπ : Function.Surjective π)
157 (n : ℕ) : (pbwFiltration π hπ).F n = pbwPiece π n :=
158 rfl

Bracket identity. The commutator of two generators is the generator of their Lie bracket, [genx,geny]=gen[x,y]; in particular it lies in F1​.

160/-- **Bracket identity.** The commutator of two generators is the generator of their Lie
161bracket, `[gen x, gen y] = gen [x, y]`; in particular it lies in `F₁`. -/
162theorem gen_bracket (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R) (x y : L) :
163 gen π x * gen π y - gen π y * gen π x = gen π ⁅x, y⁆ := by
164 have h : ι k ⁅x, y⁆ = ι k x * ι k y - ι k y * ι k x := by
165 rw [LieHom.map_lie, Ring.lie_def]
166 simp only [gen, h, map_sub, map_mul]

Reordering lemma. Permuting the factors of a word changes its product only by an element of the filtration piece Fn​ as soon as the word has length at most n+1. The proof moves one adjacent transposition at a time; a swap changes the product by −[genx,geny]⋅(rest), which lies in F1​⋅Flen rest​⊆Flen rest+1​.

168/-- Reordering lemma. Permuting the factors of a word changes its product only by an element of
169the filtration piece `Fₙ` as soon as the word has length at most `n + 1`. The proof moves one
170adjacent transposition at a time; a swap changes the product by `-[gen x, gen y] · (rest)`,
171which lies in `F₁ · F_{len rest} ⊆ F_{len rest + 1}`. -/
172theorem wordProd_sub_mem_pbwPiece (π : UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra k L →ₐ[k] R)
173 {w w' : List L} (h : w.Perm w') :
174 ∀ n : ℕ, w.length ≤ n + 1 → wordProd π w - wordProd π w' ∈ pbwPiece π n := by
175 induction h with
176 | nil =>
177 intro n _
178 simp [wordProd_nil]
179 | @cons x l₁ l₂ hl ih =>
180 intro n hn
181 cases n with
182 | zero =>
183 have h1 : l₁ = [] := List.length_eq_zero_iff.mp (by simp only [List.length_cons] at hn; omega)
184 have h2 : l₂ = [] := List.length_eq_zero_iff.mp (by rw [← hl.length_eq, h1]; rfl)
185 subst h1 h2
186 simp
187 | succ m =>
188 simp only [wordProd_cons, ← mul_sub]
189 have hm := ih m (by simp only [List.length_cons] at hn; omega)
190 exact pbwPiece_mono π (by omega) (mul_mem_pbwPiece π (gen_mem_pbwPiece_one π x) hm)
191 | @swap x y l =>
192 intro n hn
193 simp only [List.length_cons] at hn
194 simp only [wordProd_cons]
195 have e : gen π y * (gen π x * wordProd π l) - gen π x * (gen π y * wordProd π l) =
196 -(gen π ⁅x, y⁆ * wordProd π l) := by
197 rw [← gen_bracket π x y]
198 noncomm_ring
199 rw [e]
200 exact Submodule.neg_mem _ (pbwPiece_mono π (by omega)
201 (mul_mem_pbwPiece π (gen_mem_pbwPiece_one π ⁅x, y⁆) (wordProd_mem_pbwPiece π le_rfl)))
202 | @trans w₁ w₂ w₃ h₁ h₂ ih₁ ih₂ =>
203 intro n hn
204 have hn' : w₂.length ≤ n + 1 := by rw [← h₁.length_eq]; exact hn
205 have := Submodule.add_mem _ (ih₁ n hn) (ih₂ n hn')
206 simpa using this
