Simplicity of Dk​(R)

Call an ideal J of the nontrivial commutative k-algebra R differential if DJ⊆J for every k-derivation D of R. If the only nonzero differential ideal of R is R itself, then Dk​(R) is a simple ring (isSimpleRing_of_derivation). No coordinates and no characteristic assumption are needed.

Proof

Let I be a nonzero two-sided ideal of D=Dk​(R) and P∈I nonzero of minimal order. The commutators [P, a] = P a - a P (a∈R) lie in I and have smaller order, so they vanish, and P is multiplication by some f=0 (exists_multiplication_mem). The multipliers J=f∈R∣f∈I form a nonzero ideal of R, which is differential because [D, f] = D(f). Hence J=R, 1∈I and I=D.

In characteristic zero the hypothesis holds for polynomial rings and their localizations (LocalizedPolynomialOperators) and for smooth domains (SmoothSimplicity). The argument is AA-native; compare [McConnell–Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 15.3.8] and [Coutinho, A Primer of Algebraic D-modules, 2.2.1].

DifferentialOperators.infinite_center: over an infinite field (in particular in characteristic zero), Dk​(R) has infinite centre.

**A nonzero two-sided ideal of Dk​(R) contains a nonzero multiplication.** Induction on the order: if all [P, a] vanish, P is a multiplication; otherwise some [P, a] ∈ I is nonzero of smaller order. No coordinates are needed.

43/-- **A nonzero two-sided ideal of `D_k(R)` contains a nonzero multiplication.**
44Induction on the order: if all `[P, a]` vanish, `P` is a multiplication;
45otherwise some `[P, a] ∈ I` is nonzero of smaller order. No coordinates are
46needed. -/
47theorem exists_multiplication_mem (I : TwoSidedIdeal (algebra (k := k) (R := R))) :
48 ∀ m, ∀ P ∈ I, P ≠ 0 → (P : End (k := k) (R := R)) ∈ order m →
49 ∃ f : R, f ≠ 0 ∧ multiplicationAlgHom k R f ∈ I := by
50 intro m
51 induction m with
52 | zero =>
53 intro P hPI hP0 hP
54 have hPf : multiplicationAlgHom k R (P.1 1) = P :=
55 Subtype.ext ((mem_order_zero_iff_eq_multiplication _).mp hP).symm
56 refine ⟨P.1 1, fun h => hP0 ?_, by rw [hPf]; exact hPI⟩
57 rw [← hPf, h, map_zero]
58 | succ m ih =>
59 intro P hPI hP0 hP
60 by_cases hcomm : ∀ a, P * multiplicationAlgHom k R a -
61 multiplicationAlgHom k R a * P = 0
62 · exact ih P hPI hP0 (order_mono (Nat.zero_le m) fun a => congrArg Subtype.val (hcomm a))
63 · push Not at hcomm
64 obtain ⟨a, ha⟩ := hcomm
65 exact ih _ (I.sub_mem (I.mul_mem_right _ _ hPI) (I.mul_mem_left _ _ hPI)) ha
66 ((mem_order_succ_iff _ m).mp hP a)

Simplicity criterion. Dk​(R) is simple if R is nontrivial and has no nonzero proper ideal stable under all k-derivations.

68/-- **Simplicity criterion.** `D_k(R)` is simple if `R` is nontrivial and has
69no nonzero proper ideal stable under all `k`-derivations. -/
70theorem isSimpleRing_of_derivation [Nontrivial R]
71 (hR : ∀ J : Ideal R, J ≠ ⊥ → (∀ D : Derivation k R R, ∀ f ∈ J, D f ∈ J) → J = ⊤) :
72 IsSimpleRing (algebra (k := k) (R := R)) := by
73 refine IsSimpleRing.of_eq_bot_or_eq_top fun I => ?_
74 by_cases hI : I = ⊥
75 · exact Or.inl hI
76 right
77 obtain ⟨P, hPI, hP0⟩ : ∃ P ∈ I, P ≠ 0 := by
78 by_contra h
79 push Not at h
80 exact hI (eq_bot_iff.mpr fun P hP => (TwoSidedIdeal.mem_bot _).mpr (h P hP))
81 obtain ⟨m, hm⟩ := P.2
82 obtain ⟨f, hf0, hfI⟩ := exists_multiplication_mem I m P hPI hP0 hm
83 let J : Ideal R :=
84 { carrier := {g | multiplicationAlgHom k R g ∈ I}
85 add_mem' := fun {a b} ha hb => by
86 simp only [Set.mem_ofPred_eq, map_add] at ha hb ⊢
87 exact I.add_mem ha hb
88 zero_mem' := by simp only [Set.mem_ofPred_eq, map_zero]; exact I.zero_mem
89 smul_mem' := fun c g hg => by
90 simp only [Set.mem_ofPred_eq, smul_eq_mul, map_mul] at hg ⊢
91 exact I.mul_mem_left _ _ hg }
92 have hJ : J = ⊤ := by
93 refine hR J (fun h => hf0 ?_) (fun D g hg => ?_)
94 · have hfJ : f ∈ J := hfI
95 rwa [h, Ideal.mem_bot] at hfJ
96 · have hD : multiplicationAlgHom k R (D g) =
97 derivationElem D * multiplicationAlgHom k R g -
98 multiplicationAlgHom k R g * derivationElem D :=
99 Subtype.ext (commutator_derivation D g).symm
100 change multiplicationAlgHom k R (D g) ∈ I
101 rw [hD]
102 exact I.sub_mem (I.mul_mem_left _ _ hg) (I.mul_mem_right _ _ hg)
103 have h1 : (1 : R) ∈ J := hJ ▸ Submodule.mem_top
104 have h1' : multiplicationAlgHom k R 1 ∈ I := h1
105 rw [map_one] at h1'
106 exact (TwoSidedIdeal.one_mem_iff I).mp h1'
107
108end Criterion
