Differential operators when ΩR/k​ is projective

A projective frame of the commutative k-algebra R (DifferentialOperators.IsProjectiveFrame x E) consists of algebra generators x1​,…,xr​ of R and derivations E1​,…,Er​ with

db=∑i​Ei​(b)dxi​ in ΩR/k​ for every b∈R.

Equivalently, ω↦(Ei​ω)i​ splits the surjection Rr↠ΩR/k​, ei​↦dxi​. Unlike a coordinate frame, the Ei​ need not be dual to the xi​; the matrix Ei​(xj​) is only idempotent. A finitely generated algebra has a projective frame exactly when ΩR/k​ is projective (exists_isProjectiveFrame), for instance when R is smooth over k.

Main results

  • IsProjectiveFrame.derivation_eq_sum: every derivation D:R→M into an R-module satisfies Db=∑i​Ei​(b)D(xi​).
  • IsProjectiveFrame.nsmul_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow (Euler identity modulo lower order, any characteristic): for P of order at most m, m P - ∑ᵢ [P, xᵢ] Eᵢ has order at most m−1.
  • Characteristic zero: the order filtration of Dk​(R) is generated in degree one by the Ei​ over R (IsProjectiveFrame.polynomialGenerators), so R[ξ1​,…,ξr​]↠grDk​(R). Hence Dk​(R) is generated by R and the Ei​, and if R is Noetherian of Krull dimension at most d, Dk​(R) is right and left Noetherian with deviation at most d+r on both sides.
  • exists_isProjectiveFrame, ringKrullDim_le_of_adjoin_eq_top: a finitely generated algebra with projective Ω generated by r elements has a projective frame of length r and Krull dimension at most r, so (devLE_right_of_adjoin_eq_top, devLE_left_of_adjoin_eq_top) Dk​(R) has deviation at most 2r.

Proof of the Euler identity

The map b ↦ [P, b] is a derivation modulo lower order: for P of order at most n+1 the classes of [P, b] in ordern/orderBelown satisfy Leibniz, since [P, a] b - b [P, a] = [[P, a], b] drops the order. Therefore [P, b] ≡ ∑ᵢ Eᵢ(b) [P, xᵢ] (commutator_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow). The induction then runs as for coordinate frames (IsCoordinateFrame.nsmul_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow), using the commutator test on algebra generators.

References

[EGA IV] A. Grothendieck, Éléments de géométrie algébrique IV, 16.11.2 (differential operators of a differentially smooth algebra). The argument via projective frames is AA-native.

12# Differential operators when `Ω_{R/k}` is projective
13
14A **projective frame** of the commutative `k`-algebra `R`
15(`DifferentialOperators.IsProjectiveFrame x E`) consists of algebra generators
16`x₁, …, x_r` of `R` and derivations `E₁, …, E_r` with
17
18 `d b = ∑ᵢ Eᵢ(b) d xᵢ` in `Ω_{R/k}` for every `b ∈ R`.
19
20Equivalently, `ω ↦ (Eᵢ ω)ᵢ` splits the surjection `Rʳ ↠ Ω_{R/k}`,
21`eᵢ ↦ d xᵢ`. Unlike a coordinate frame, the `Eᵢ` need not be dual to the
22`xᵢ`; the matrix `Eᵢ(xⱼ)` is only idempotent. A finitely generated algebra has
23a projective frame exactly when `Ω_{R/k}` is projective
24(`exists_isProjectiveFrame`), for instance when `R` is smooth over `k`.
25
26## Main results
27
28* `IsProjectiveFrame.derivation_eq_sum`: every derivation `D : R → M` into an
29 `R`-module satisfies `D b = ∑ᵢ Eᵢ(b) D(xᵢ)`.
30* `IsProjectiveFrame.nsmul_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow` (**Euler identity modulo
31 lower order**, any characteristic): for `P` of order at most `m`,
32 `m P - ∑ᵢ [P, xᵢ] Eᵢ` has order at most `m - 1`.
33* Characteristic zero: the order filtration of `D_k(R)` is generated in degree
34 one by the `Eᵢ` over `R` (`IsProjectiveFrame.polynomialGenerators`), so
35 `R[ξ₁, …, ξ_r] ↠ gr D_k(R)`. Hence `D_k(R)` is generated by `R` and the
36 `Eᵢ`, and if `R` is Noetherian of Krull dimension at most `d`, `D_k(R)` is
37 right and left Noetherian with deviation at most `d + r` on both sides.
38* `exists_isProjectiveFrame`, `ringKrullDim_le_of_adjoin_eq_top`: a finitely
39 generated algebra with projective `Ω` generated by `r` elements has a
40 projective frame of length `r` and Krull dimension at most `r`, so
41 (`devLE_right_of_adjoin_eq_top`, `devLE_left_of_adjoin_eq_top`) `D_k(R)` has
42 deviation at most `2r`.

44## Proof of the Euler identity
45
46The map `b ↦ [P, b]` is a derivation modulo lower order: for `P` of order at
47most `n + 1` the classes of `[P, b]` in `order n / orderBelow n` satisfy
48Leibniz, since `[P, a] b - b [P, a] = [[P, a], b]` drops the order. Therefore
49`[P, b] ≡ ∑ᵢ Eᵢ(b) [P, xᵢ]` (`commutator_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow`). The
50induction then runs as for coordinate frames
51(`IsCoordinateFrame.nsmul_sub_sum_mem_orderBelow`), using the commutator test
52on algebra generators.
