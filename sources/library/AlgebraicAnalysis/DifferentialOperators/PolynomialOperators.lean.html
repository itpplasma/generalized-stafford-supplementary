Differential operators on a polynomial ring

Let k be a field and R=k[x1​,…,xn​]. This file applies the coordinate symbol presentation (DifferentialOperators.CoordinateSymbol) to the coordinates xi​ and the partial derivatives ∂i​=∂/∂xi​, and identifies the ring Dk​(R) of all finite-order k-linear differential operators with the Weyl algebra An​(k) in characteristic zero.

Main results

For a field k of characteristic zero, D=Dk​(k[x1​,…,xn​]):

  • PolynomialOperators.isNoetherian_right, isNoetherianRing: D is right and left Noetherian.
  • PolynomialOperators.devLE_right, devLE_left: the right and left ideals of D have deviation at most 2n; devLEOmega_right, devLEOmega_left.
  • PolynomialOperators.adjoin_eq_top: D is generated by the multiplications by xi​ and the ∂i​.
  • PolynomialOperators.weylEquiv : WeylAlg k n ≃ₐ[k] D, xi​↦xi​, ∂i​↦∂/∂xi​. Surjectivity is the generation theorem; injectivity holds because An​(k) is simple (Weyl.Simplicity.isSimpleRing_weylAlg).
  • PolynomialOperators.isSimpleRing (transported from An​(k)), isDomain (from the principal symbol, IsCoordinateFrame.isDomain), infinite_center.

The Noetherian and deviation statements are proved from the order filtration of D itself, not transported from the Weyl algebra.

12# Differential operators on a polynomial ring
13
14Let `k` be a field and `R = k[x₁, …, xₙ]`. This file applies the coordinate
15symbol presentation (`DifferentialOperators.CoordinateSymbol`) to the
16coordinates `xᵢ` and the partial derivatives `∂ᵢ = ∂/∂xᵢ`, and identifies the
17ring `D_k(R)` of all finite-order `k`-linear differential operators with the
18Weyl algebra `A_n(k)` in characteristic zero.

20## Main results
21
22For a field `k` of characteristic zero, `D = D_k(k[x₁, …, xₙ])`:
23
24* `PolynomialOperators.isNoetherian_right`, `isNoetherianRing`: `D` is right
25 and left Noetherian.
26* `PolynomialOperators.devLE_right`, `devLE_left`: the right and left ideals
27 of `D` have deviation at most `2n`; `devLEOmega_right`, `devLEOmega_left`.
28* `PolynomialOperators.adjoin_eq_top`: `D` is generated by the
29 multiplications by `xᵢ` and the `∂ᵢ`.

31 `∂ᵢ ↦ ∂/∂xᵢ`. Surjectivity is the generation theorem; injectivity holds
32 because `A_n(k)` is simple (`Weyl.Simplicity.isSimpleRing_weylAlg`).
33* `PolynomialOperators.isSimpleRing` (transported from `A_n(k)`), `isDomain`

54 pderiv i (X j : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k) = if i = j then 1 else 0 := by

Partial derivatives commute.

58/-- Partial derivatives commute. -/
59theorem pderiv_mul_pderiv_comm (i j : Fin n) :
60 (pderiv (R := k) i).toLinearMap * (pderiv (R := k) j).toLinearMap =
61 (pderiv (R := k) j).toLinearMap * (pderiv (R := k) i).toLinearMap := by
62 have h : ⁅(pderiv i : Derivation k (MvPolynomial (Fin n) k) (MvPolynomial (Fin n) k)),
63 pderiv (R := k) j⁆ = 0 := by
64 apply MvPolynomial.derivation_ext
65 intro l
66 simp only [Derivation.commutator_apply, Derivation.zero_apply, pderiv_X_eq_ite]
67 split_ifs <;> simp
68 refine LinearMap.ext fun f => ?_
69 have hf := Derivation.congr_fun h f
70 simp only [Derivation.commutator_apply, Derivation.zero_apply] at hf
71 exact sub_eq_zero.mp hf

130/-- The Weyl relations `[∂ᵢ, xⱼ] = δᵢⱼ`, `[xᵢ, xⱼ] = [∂ᵢ, ∂ⱼ] = 0`, in the sign

157@[simp] theorem fromWeyl_generator (i : PhaseVar n) :
158 fromWeyl k n (generator k n i) = weylGenerator k n i :=
159 Universal.freeWeylLift_generator _ _ _ i

161theorem fromWeyl_surjective : Function.Surjective (fromWeyl k n) := by
162 rw [← AlgHom.range_eq_top, eq_top_iff, ← adjoin_eq_top, Algebra.adjoin_le_iff]
163 rintro _ (⟨a, rfl⟩ | ⟨i, rfl⟩)
164 · have hX : Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range (X : Fin n → MvPolynomial (Fin n) k)) ≤
165 (fromWeyl k n).range.comap (multiplicationAlgHom k _) := by
166 rw [Algebra.adjoin_le_iff]
167 rintro _ ⟨i, rfl⟩
168 exact ⟨generator k n (.inl i), fromWeyl_generator _⟩
169 rw [adjoin_range_X'] at hX
170 exact hX (Algebra.mem_top (x := a))
171 · exact ⟨generator k n (.inr i), fromWeyl_generator _⟩
172
173theorem fromWeyl_injective : Function.Injective (fromWeyl k n) := by
174 have := Simplicity.isSimpleRing_weylAlg k n
175 exact (fromWeyl k n).toRingHom.injective
