Differential operators on a localized polynomial ring

Let k be a field, S a submonoid of k[x1​,…,xn​] and B=S−1k[x1​,…,xn​] (any IsLocalization S B). Examples: k[x][f−1] (powers of f), the Laurent polynomials, and the rational function field k(x1​,…,xn​) (all nonzero polynomials). The images of the coordinates xi​ and the extended partial derivatives ∂i​ (LocalizedPolynomialDerivations.localizedPderiv) form a coordinate frame of B (isCoordinateFrame), so the general coordinate theory applies to D=Dk​(B).

Main results

In characteristic zero:

  • isNoetherian_right, isNoetherianRing: D is right and left Noetherian.
  • devLE_right, devLE_left: right and left ideals of D have deviation at most 2n (since dimB≤n, ringKrullDim_le); devLEOmega_right, devLEOmega_left.
  • adjoin_eq_top: D is generated by B and the ∂i​.

If moreover 0∈/S (S ≤ nonZeroDivisors):

  • eq_top_of_pderiv_mem: the only nonzero ideal of B stable under all ∂i​ is B (lower the total degree of a numerator with the ∂i​).
  • isSimpleRing: D is simple.
  • isDomain: D is a domain (grD≅B[ξ]).
  • infinite_center: D has infinite centre.

Also AlgebraicAnalysis.ringKrullDim_le_of_isLocalization: localization does not increase Krull dimension.

The arguments are AA-native; for the statements see [McConnell–Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 15.1.20, 15.3.8].

Localization does not increase Krull dimension: primes of S−1A embed order-preservingly into primes of A by contraction.

48/-- **Localization does not increase Krull dimension**: primes of `S⁻¹A`
49embed order-preservingly into primes of `A` by contraction. -/
50theorem ringKrullDim_le_of_isLocalization {A B : Type*} [CommRing A] [CommRing B] [Algebra A B]
51 (S : Submonoid A) [IsLocalization S B] : ringKrullDim B ≤ ringKrullDim A :=
52 Order.krullDim_le_of_strictMono
53 (fun p => ⟨p.asIdeal.under A, Ideal.IsPrime.under A p.asIdeal⟩)
54 fun _ _ h => (IsLocalization.orderEmbedding S B).strictMono h
55

A partial derivative lowers the total degree by at least one.

68/-- A partial derivative lowers the total degree by at least one. -/
69theorem totalDegree_pderiv_le (p : MvPolynomial σ k) (i : σ) :
70 (pderiv i p).totalDegree ≤ p.totalDegree - 1 := by
71 refine Finset.sup_le fun m hm => ?_
72 rw [mem_support_iff, coeff_pderiv] at hm
73 have hc := left_ne_zero_of_mul hm
74 have hle := le_totalDegree (mem_support_iff.mpr hc)
75 rw [Finsupp.sum_add_index' (fun _ => rfl) (fun _ _ _ => rfl),
76 Finsupp.sum_single_index rfl] at hle
77 omega

In characteristic zero, a polynomial with all partial derivatives zero is constant.

81/-- In characteristic zero, a polynomial with all partial derivatives zero is
82constant. -/
83theorem eq_C_of_pderiv_eq_zero {p : MvPolynomial σ k} (h : ∀ i, pderiv i p = 0) :
84 p = C (p.coeff 0) := by
85 classical
86 ext m
87 by_cases hm : m = 0
88 · subst hm
89 simp
90 obtain ⟨i, hi⟩ : ∃ i, m i ≠ 0 := by
91 by_contra hcon
92 push Not at hcon
93 exact hm (Finsupp.ext hcon)
94 rw [coeff_C]
95 simp only [Ne.symm hm, ↓reduceIte]
96 have hm' : m - Finsupp.single i 1 + Finsupp.single i 1 = m := by
97 ext l
98 simp only [Finsupp.coe_add, Finsupp.coe_tsub, Pi.add_apply, Pi.sub_apply,
99 Finsupp.single_apply]
100 split_ifs with hl
101 · subst hl
102 omega
103 · simp
104 have hcoeff := coeff_pderiv (i := i) p (m - Finsupp.single i 1)
105 rw [h i, hm'] at hcoeff
106 have hne : (((m - Finsupp.single i 1 : σ →₀ ℕ) i : ℕ) : k) + 1 ≠ 0 := Nat.cast_add_one_ne_zero _
107 rcases mul_eq_zero.mp (hcoeff.symm.trans (by simp)) with h0 | h0
108 · exact h0
109 · exact absurd h0 hne
110
111end Pderiv

**The coordinates and the extended partial derivatives form a coordinate frame of B=S−1k[x]**: every element is r/s with r,s polynomials and s a unit, and ∂i​(xj​)=δij​.

143/-- **The coordinates and the extended partial derivatives form a coordinate
144frame of `B = S⁻¹k[x]`**: every element is `r/s` with `r, s` polynomials and
145`s` a unit, and `∂ᵢ(xⱼ) = δᵢⱼ`. -/

147 IsCoordinateFrame (coordinate (k := k) (n := n) B) (localizedPderiv S B) where

150 exact ⟨_, algebraMap_mem_adjoin B r, _, algebraMap_mem_adjoin B s,
151 IsLocalization.map_units B s, hrs⟩

**The only nonzero ideal of B stable under all ∂i​ is B.** A nonzero b∈J has a nonzero polynomial numerator r=bs∈J; applying partial derivatives lowers its total degree until a nonzero constant, a unit, lies in J.

212/-- **The only nonzero ideal of `B` stable under all `∂ᵢ` is `B`.** A nonzero
213`b ∈ J` has a nonzero polynomial numerator `r = b s ∈ J`; applying partial
214derivatives lowers its total degree until a nonzero constant, a unit, lies
215in `J`. -/
216theorem eq_top_of_pderiv_mem (J : Ideal B) (hJ : J ≠ ⊥)
217 (hD : ∀ i, ∀ f ∈ J, localizedPderiv S B i f ∈ J) : J = ⊤ := by
218 have hinj := IsLocalization.injective B hS
219 have hpd : ∀ i (p : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k),
220 localizedPderiv S B i (algebraMap _ B p) = algebraMap _ B (pderiv i p) :=
221 fun i p => Derivation.congr_fun (localizedPderiv_compAlgebraMap S B i) p
222 have key : ∀ d, ∀ p : MvPolynomial (Fin n) k, p.totalDegree ≤ d → p ≠ 0 →
223 algebraMap _ B p ∈ J → J = ⊤ := by
224 intro d
225 induction d with
226 | zero =>
227 intro p hp hp0 hpJ
228 obtain ⟨c, rfl⟩ : ∃ c, p = C c := ⟨_, totalDegree_eq_zero_iff_eq_C.mp (by omega)⟩
229 have hc : c ≠ 0 := fun h => hp0 (by rw [h, C_0])
230 rw [← MvPolynomial.algebraMap_eq, ← IsScalarTower.algebraMap_apply] at hpJ
231 exact Ideal.eq_top_of_isUnit_mem J hpJ ((IsUnit.mk0 c hc).map (algebraMap k B))
232 | succ d ih =>
233 intro p hp hp0 hpJ
234 by_cases hall : ∀ i, pderiv i p = 0
235 · rw [eq_C_of_pderiv_eq_zero hall] at hpJ hp0
236 exact ih _ (by simp) hp0 hpJ
237 · push Not at hall
238 obtain ⟨i, hi⟩ := hall
239 refine ih _ ((totalDegree_pderiv_le p i).trans (by omega)) hi ?_
240 rw [← hpd]
241 exact hD i _ hpJ
242 obtain ⟨b, hbJ, hb0⟩ := Submodule.exists_mem_ne_zero_of_ne_bot hJ
243 obtain ⟨⟨r, s⟩, hrs⟩ := IsLocalization.surj S b
244 refine key _ r le_rfl (fun hr => hb0 ?_) (hrs ▸ J.mul_mem_right _ hbJ)
245 rw [hr, map_zero] at hrs
246 exact (IsLocalization.map_units B s).mul_left_eq_zero.mp hrs
