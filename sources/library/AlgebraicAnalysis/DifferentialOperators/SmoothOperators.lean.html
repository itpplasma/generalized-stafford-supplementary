9 # Differential operators on a smooth affine variety
10 
11 Let `k` be a field of characteristic zero and `A` a smooth `k`-algebra
12 (`Algebra.Smooth k A`; finitely presented with projective `Ω_{A/k}`), and let
13 `D = D_k(A)` be the ring of finite-order `k`-linear differential operators
14 (`DifferentialOperators.algebra`).
15 
16 ## Main results
17 
18 * `isNoetherian_right`, `isNoetherianRing`: `D` is right and left Noetherian.
19 * `devLE_right`, `devLE_left`: if `A` is generated by `r` elements, the right
20   and left ideals of `D` have deviation at most `2r`; `devLEOmega_right`,
21   `devLEOmega_left`: deviation at most omega.
22 * `adjoin_eq_top`: `D` is generated by the multiplications and finitely many
23   derivations.
24 
25 If moreover `A` is a domain:
26 
27 * `isDomain`: `D` is a domain.
28 * `isSimpleRing`: `D` is simple.
29 * `infinite_center`: `D` has infinite centre.
30 
31 ## Proof outline
32 
33 * Noetherian and deviation: generators `x₁, …, x_r` of `A` extend to a
34   projective frame (`exists_isProjectiveFrame`), whose Euler identity presents
35   `gr D` as a quotient of `A[ξ₁, …, ξ_r]` (`IsProjectiveFrame.polynomialGenerators`);
36   `dim A ≤ r` (`ringKrullDim_le_of_adjoin_eq_top`).
37 * Domain: polarization of the principal symbol (`DifferentialOperators.isDomain`),
38   valid for every domain.
39 * Simplicity: a smooth domain has no nonzero proper ideal stable under all
40   derivations (`eq_top_of_derivation_mem_of_smooth`), by quasi-finiteness over
41   a polynomial ring on standard smooth opens; then
42   `isSimpleRing_of_derivation`.
43 
44 No axioms beyond Lean's standard ones are used. Compare [McConnell–Robson,
45 *Noncommutative Noetherian Rings*, 15.1.20, 15.3.8, 15.5.5] and
46 [EGA IV, 16.11.2].

56 /-- The ring `D_k(A)` of finite-order `k`-linear differential operators on `A`. -/
57 abbrev DiffOp : Type v := algebra (k := k) (R := A)

59 omit [CharZero k] in
60 /-- `A` has a projective frame indexed by a finite set of algebra generators. -/
61 theorem exists_isProjectiveFrame' :
62     ∃ r, ∃ x : Fin r → A, Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range x) = ⊤ ∧
63       ∃ E : Fin r → Derivation k A A, IsProjectiveFrame x E := by
64   obtain ⟨r, x, hx⟩ : ∃ r, ∃ x : Fin r → A, Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range x) = ⊤ := by
65     obtain ⟨s, hs⟩ := (inferInstance : Algebra.FiniteType k A).out
66     refine ⟨s.card, fun i => (s.equivFin.symm i : A), ?_⟩
67     rw [← hs]
68     congr 1
69     ext a
70     exact ⟨fun ⟨i, hi⟩ => hi ▸ (s.equivFin.symm i).2, fun ha => ⟨s.equivFin ⟨a, ha⟩, by simp⟩⟩
71   exact ⟨r, x, hx, exists_isProjectiveFrame hx⟩
72 
73 /-- **`D_k(A)` is generated by `A` and finitely many derivations.** -/
74 theorem adjoin_eq_top : ∃ r, ∃ E : Fin r → Derivation k A A,
75     Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range (multiplicationAlgHom k A) ∪
76       Set.range fun i => derivationElem (E i)) = ⊤ := by
77   obtain ⟨r, x, -, E, hF⟩ := exists_isProjectiveFrame' k A
78   exact ⟨r, E, hF.adjoin_eq_top_diffOp⟩

80 /-- **`D_k(A)` is right Noetherian.** -/
81 theorem isNoetherian_right : IsNoetherian (DiffOp k A)ᵐᵒᵖ (DiffOp k A) := by
82   have := Algebra.FiniteType.isNoetherianRing k A
83   obtain ⟨r, x, -, E, hF⟩ := exists_isProjectiveFrame' k A
84   exact hF.isNoetherian_right
