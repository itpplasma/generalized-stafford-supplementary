Differential operators on a smooth affine variety

Let k be a field of characteristic zero and A a smooth k-algebra (Algebra.Smooth k A; finitely presented with projective ΩA/k​), and let D=Dk​(A) be the ring of finite-order k-linear differential operators (DifferentialOperators.algebra).

Main results

  • isNoetherian_right, isNoetherianRing: D is right and left Noetherian.
  • devLE_right, devLE_left: if A is generated by r elements, the right and left ideals of D have deviation at most 2r; devLEOmega_right, devLEOmega_left: deviation at most omega.
  • adjoin_eq_top: D is generated by the multiplications and finitely many derivations.

If moreover A is a domain:

  • isDomain: D is a domain.
  • isSimpleRing: D is simple.
  • infinite_center: D has infinite centre.

Proof outline

  • Noetherian and deviation: generators x1​,…,xr​ of A extend to a projective frame (exists_isProjectiveFrame), whose Euler identity presents grD as a quotient of A[ξ1​,…,ξr​] (IsProjectiveFrame.polynomialGenerators); dimA≤r (ringKrullDim_le_of_adjoin_eq_top).
  • Domain: polarization of the principal symbol (DifferentialOperators.isDomain), valid for every domain.
  • Simplicity: a smooth domain has no nonzero proper ideal stable under all derivations (eq_top_of_derivation_mem_of_smooth), by quasi-finiteness over a polynomial ring on standard smooth opens; then isSimpleRing_of_derivation.

No axioms beyond Lean's standard ones are used. Compare [McConnell–Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 15.1.20, 15.3.8, 15.5.5] and [EGA IV, 16.11.2].

A has a projective frame indexed by a finite set of algebra generators.

60/-- `A` has a projective frame indexed by a finite set of algebra generators. -/
61theorem exists_isProjectiveFrame' :
62 ∃ r, ∃ x : Fin r → A, Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range x) = ⊤ ∧
63 ∃ E : Fin r → Derivation k A A, IsProjectiveFrame x E := by
64 obtain ⟨r, x, hx⟩ : ∃ r, ∃ x : Fin r → A, Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range x) = ⊤ := by
65 obtain ⟨s, hs⟩ := (inferInstance : Algebra.FiniteType k A).out
66 refine ⟨s.card, fun i => (s.equivFin.symm i : A), ?_⟩
67 rw [← hs]
68 congr 1
69 ext a
70 exact ⟨fun ⟨i, hi⟩ => hi ▸ (s.equivFin.symm i).2, fun ha => ⟨s.equivFin ⟨a, ha⟩, by simp⟩⟩
71 exact ⟨r, x, hx, exists_isProjectiveFrame hx⟩

**Dk​(A) is generated by A and finitely many derivations.**

73/-- **`D_k(A)` is generated by `A` and finitely many derivations.** -/
74theorem adjoin_eq_top : ∃ r, ∃ E : Fin r → Derivation k A A,
75 Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range (multiplicationAlgHom k A) ∪
76 Set.range fun i => derivationElem (E i)) = ⊤ := by
77 obtain ⟨r, x, -, E, hF⟩ := exists_isProjectiveFrame' k A
78 exact ⟨r, E, hF.adjoin_eq_top_diffOp⟩
