Locally small modules and dense submodules

All modules are left modules over an arbitrary ring; use the opposite ring for right modules. Local smallness tests finitely generated submodules, rather than asserting a deviation bound on an infinite direct sum. Extension closure uses a Noetherian scalar ring. The small radical is small for Noetherian modules.

These are new proofs for the interfaces in generalized-stafford's research notes docs/intrinsic-finite-krull.md, §2, and .lake/research/active/formal-scope-BC.md, steps 2 and 4; no source-code extraction or downstream declaration replacement is involved.

A finitely generated submodule of a sum of small submodules is small.

87/-- A finitely generated submodule of a sum of small submodules is small. -/
88theorem small_of_fg_le_iSup {ι : Type*} (U : ι → Submodule R M)
89 (hU : ∀ i, Small R e (U i)) {V : Submodule R M} (hV : V.FG)
90 (hVU : V ≤ ⨆ i, U i) : Small R e V := by
91 classical
92 obtain ⟨s, hs⟩ := (Submodule.fg_iff_compact V).mp hV |>.exists_finset_of_le_iSup U hVU
93 rw [← Finset.sup_eq_iSup] at hs
94 exact (Small.finset_sup s U (fun i _ => hU i)).of_le hs

96theorem locSmall_smallRadical : LocSmall R e (smallRadical R e M) := by

Pointwise small envelopes are enough: finite generators use a finite sum of them.

128/-- Pointwise small envelopes are enough: finite generators use a finite sum of them. -/
129theorem locSmall_of_small_envelopes
130 (h : ∀ x : M, ∃ U : Submodule R M, Small R e U ∧ x ∈ U) : LocSmall R e M := by
131 have ht : smallRadical R e M = ⊤ := by
132 apply top_unique
133 intro x _
134 obtain ⟨U, hU, hx⟩ := h x
135 exact le_smallRadical hU hx
136 intro U hU
137 exact small_of_fg_le_iSup
138 (fun V : {V : Submodule R M // Small R e V} => V.val) (fun V => V.property)
139 hU (by change U ≤ smallRadical R e M; rw [ht]; exact le_top)

141theorem LocSmall.small_envelope (h : LocSmall R e M) (x : M) :
142 ∃ U : Submodule R M, Small R e U ∧ x ∈ U :=
143 ⟨R ∙ x, h _ (Submodule.fg_span_singleton x), Submodule.mem_span_singleton_self x⟩

The Noetherian ring hypothesis controls the kernel inside each finite submodule.

162/-- The Noetherian ring hypothesis controls the kernel inside each finite submodule. -/
163theorem LocSmall.of_submodule_quotient [IsNoetherianRing R] (U : Submodule R M)
164 (hU : LocSmall R e U) (hQ : LocSmall R e (M ⧸ U)) : LocSmall R e M := by
165 intro V hV
166 have : Module.Finite R V := Module.Finite.of_fg hV
167 let f : V →ₗ[R] M ⧸ U := U.mkQ.comp V.subtype
168 have hk : Small R e (LinearMap.ker f) := by
169 have hh := hU.of_injective
170 (show LinearMap.ker f →ₗ[R] U from
171 { toFun := fun x => ⟨x.val.val, (Submodule.Quotient.mk_eq_zero U).mp x.property⟩
172 map_add' := fun _ _ => rfl
173 map_smul' := fun _ _ => rfl })
174 (by intro x y h; apply Subtype.ext; apply Subtype.ext; have hh := congrArg Subtype.val h; exact hh)
175 exact locSmall_iff_small.mp hh

Arbitrary sums of locally small submodules are locally small.

180/-- Arbitrary sums of locally small submodules are locally small. -/
181theorem LocSmall.iSup {ι : Type*} (U : ι → Submodule R M)
182 (hU : ∀ i, LocSmall R e (U i)) : LocSmall R e (⨆ i, U i : Submodule R M) := by

Arbitrary direct sums, without a finite-index hypothesis.

191/-- Arbitrary direct sums, without a finite-index hypothesis. -/
192theorem LocSmall.directSum {ι : Type*} {P : ι → Type v}
193 [∀ i, AddCommGroup (P i)] [∀ i, Module R (P i)]
194 (hP : ∀ i, LocSmall R e (P i)) : LocSmall R e (⨁ i, P i) := by
195 classical
196 let U (i : ι) : Submodule R (⨁ i, P i) := LinearMap.range (DirectSum.lof R ι P i)
197 have hU : ∀ i, LocSmall R e (U i) := fun i =>
198 (hP i).of_surjective (DirectSum.lof R ι P i).rangeRestrict (by rintro ⟨y, x, rfl⟩; exact ⟨x, rfl⟩)
199 have ht : (⨆ i, U i) = ⊤ := by
200 apply top_unique
201 suffices ∀ x : (⨁ i, P i), x ∈ ⨆ i, U i from fun x _ => this x
202 intro x

204 | zero => exact Submodule.zero_mem _
