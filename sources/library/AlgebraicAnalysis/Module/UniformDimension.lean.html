Uniform dimension via independent families of submodules

The uniform (Goldie) dimension of a module M is the supremum of the numbers n such that M contains a direct sum of n nonzero submodules. We record the two one-sided comparisons as predicates on natural numbers, so that no supremum and no finiteness hypothesis enters the definition:

  • Module.UniformDimGE R M n: there are n nonzero submodules of M whose sum is direct (an iSupIndep family indexed by Finn);
  • Module.UniformDimLE R M n: there is no such family of n+1 submodules.

Right modules over a ring R are modules over Rop, so the uniform dimension of a right module M is Module.UniformDimGE Rᵐᵒᵖ M.

Main results

  • Module.UniformDimGE.le_length: a direct sum of n nonzero submodules forces n≤lengthM;
  • Module.uniformDimGE_iff_le_length, Module.uniformDimLE_iff_length_le: for a semisimple module the uniform dimension is the length;
  • Module.UniformDimGE.mono, Module.uniformDimGE_congr: monotonicity in n and invariance under linear equivalences.

References

K. R. Goodearl and R. B. Warfield, An Introduction to Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2nd ed., Chapter 5; J. C. McConnell and J. C. Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2.2.10–2.2.12. Nothing here is new.

UniformDimGE R M n: the uniform dimension of M is at least n, that is, M contains n nonzero submodules whose sum is direct.

45/-- `UniformDimGE R M n`: the uniform dimension of `M` is at least `n`, that is,
46`M` contains `n` nonzero submodules whose sum is direct. -/
47def UniformDimGE (n : ℕ) : Prop :=
48 ∃ N : Fin n → Submodule R M, iSupIndep N ∧ ∀ i, N i ≠ ⊥

UniformDimLE R M n: the uniform dimension of M is at most n, that is, M contains no direct sum of n+1 nonzero submodules.

50/-- `UniformDimLE R M n`: the uniform dimension of `M` is at most `n`, that is,
51`M` contains no direct sum of `n + 1` nonzero submodules. -/
52def UniformDimLE (n : ℕ) : Prop :=
53 ¬ UniformDimGE R M (n + 1)

57/-- Every module has uniform dimension at least `0`. -/
58theorem uniformDimGE_zero : UniformDimGE R M 0 :=
59 ⟨Fin.elim0, fun i => i.elim0, fun i => i.elim0⟩

If the uniform dimension is at least n, it is at least every k≤n.

61/-- If the uniform dimension is at least `n`, it is at least every `k ≤ n`. -/
62theorem UniformDimGE.mono {n k : ℕ} (h : UniformDimGE R M n) (hk : k ≤ n) :
63 UniformDimGE R M k := by
64 obtain ⟨N, hN, hne⟩ := h
65 exact ⟨N ∘ Fin.castLE hk, hN.comp (Fin.castLE_injective hk), fun i => hne _⟩

A direct sum of n nonzero submodules is carried along a linear equivalence.

67/-- A direct sum of `n` nonzero submodules is carried along a linear equivalence. -/
68theorem UniformDimGE.of_linearEquiv {N : Type w} [AddCommGroup N] [Module R N] (e : M ≃ₗ[R] N)
69 {n : ℕ} (h : UniformDimGE R M n) : UniformDimGE R N n := by
70 obtain ⟨K, hK, hne⟩ := h
71 let o := Submodule.orderIsoMapComap e
72 refine ⟨fun i => o (K i), (iSupIndep_map_orderIso_iff o).mpr hK, fun i h0 => hne i ?_⟩
73 exact o.injective (h0.trans (map_bot o).symm)

The uniform dimension is invariant under linear equivalences.

75/-- The uniform dimension is invariant under linear equivalences. -/
76theorem uniformDimGE_congr {N : Type w} [AddCommGroup N] [Module R N] (e : M ≃ₗ[R] N)
77 {n : ℕ} : UniformDimGE R M n ↔ UniformDimGE R N n :=
78 ⟨UniformDimGE.of_linearEquiv e, UniformDimGE.of_linearEquiv e.symm⟩

Uniform dimension is bounded by length. If M contains a direct sum of n nonzero submodules, then n≤lengthM: the direct sum embeds into M, and each summand has length at least one.

80/-- **Uniform dimension is bounded by length.** If `M` contains a direct sum of
81`n` nonzero submodules, then `n ≤ length M`: the direct sum embeds into `M`, and
82each summand has length at least one. -/
83theorem UniformDimGE.le_length {n : ℕ} (h : UniformDimGE R M n) :
84 (n : ℕ∞) ≤ Module.length R M := by
85 classical
86 obtain ⟨N, hN, hne⟩ := h
87 calc (n : ℕ∞) = ∑ _i : Fin n, (1 : ℕ∞) := by simp
88 _ ≤ ∑ i, Module.length R (N i) := Finset.sum_le_sum fun i _ => by
89 have := (Submodule.nontrivial_iff_ne_bot).mpr (hne i)
90 exact Order.one_le_iff_pos.mpr Module.length_pos
91 _ = Module.length R (Π i, N i) := (Module.length_pi_of_fintype R _).symm
92 _ = Module.length R (Π₀ i, N i) := (DFinsupp.linearEquivFunOnFintype).length_eq.symm
93 _ ≤ Module.length R M := Module.length_le_of_injective _ hN.dfinsupp_lsum_injective

Uniform dimension of a semisimple module. A semisimple module contains a direct sum of n nonzero submodules if and only if its length is at least n.

103/-- **Uniform dimension of a semisimple module.** A semisimple module contains a
104direct sum of `n` nonzero submodules if and only if its length is at least `n`. -/
105theorem uniformDimGE_iff_le_length [IsSemisimpleModule R M] {n : ℕ} :
106 UniformDimGE R M n ↔ (n : ℕ∞) ≤ Module.length R M := by
107 classical
108 refine ⟨UniformDimGE.le_length, fun hn => ?_⟩
109 obtain ⟨s, e, hs, hsimple⟩ := IsSemisimpleModule.exists_linearEquiv_dfinsupp R M
110 have hind : iSupIndep fun m : s => m.1 := (sSupIndep_iff s).mp hs
111 have hne : ∀ m : s, m.1 ≠ ⊥ := fun m =>
112 (Submodule.nontrivial_iff_ne_bot).mp (IsSimpleModule.nontrivial R m.1)
113 suffices hemb : Nonempty (Fin n ↪ s) by
114 obtain ⟨f⟩ := hemb
115 exact ⟨fun i => (f i).1, hind.comp f.injective, fun i => hne _⟩
116 rcases finite_or_infinite s with hfin | hinf
117 · cases nonempty_fintype s
118 have hlen : Module.length R M = Fintype.card s := by
119 rw [e.length_eq, (DFinsupp.linearEquivFunOnFintype).length_eq,
120 Module.length_pi_of_fintype]
121 simp
122 rw [hlen] at hn
123 norm_cast at hn
124 exact ⟨(Fin.castLEEmb hn).trans (Fintype.equivFin s).symm.toEmbedding⟩
125 · exact ⟨(Fin.valEmbedding.trans (Infinite.natEmbedding s))⟩

Uniform dimension of a semisimple module. A semisimple module contains no direct sum of n+1 nonzero submodules if and only if its length is at most n.

127/-- **Uniform dimension of a semisimple module.** A semisimple module contains no
128direct sum of `n + 1` nonzero submodules if and only if its length is at most
129`n`. -/
130theorem uniformDimLE_iff_length_le [IsSemisimpleModule R M] {n : ℕ} :
131 UniformDimLE R M n ↔ Module.length R M ≤ n := by
132 rw [UniformDimLE, uniformDimGE_iff_le_length, not_le, Nat.cast_add, Nat.cast_one,
133 ENat.lt_add_one_iff (ENat.natCast_ne_top n)]
