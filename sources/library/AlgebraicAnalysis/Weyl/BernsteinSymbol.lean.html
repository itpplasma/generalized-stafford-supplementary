Bernstein symbol of the Weyl algebra

The Bernstein filtration of An​(k) (all phase variables of weight one) has associated graded ring the polynomial ring k[x,∂] in 2n variables, graded by total degree. This file packages that identification as a RingFiltration.GradedSymbol, which yields:

  • right Noetherianity of An​(k), a second route to the tower argument; and
  • Module.DevLEOmega (A_n(k))ᵐᵒᵖ (A_n(k)), the deviation bound Kdim≤ω used by Corollary B of the paper.

The degree-N symbol is the weighted-degree-N component of the normal-form polynomial, AlgebraicAnalysis.Weyl.principalComponentOnPiece. Its kernel is the strict lower Bernstein piece, which is exactly the predecessor piece belowN of the filtration.
