The Weyl algebra is a domain

For every field k (of any characteristic) and every n, the Weyl algebra An​(k) is a domain. The Bernstein symbol identifies the associated graded ring of the Bernstein filtration with the polynomial ring k[x,∂], which is a domain with 1 of degree 0; RingFiltration.GradedSymbol.isDomain lifts this to An​(k).
