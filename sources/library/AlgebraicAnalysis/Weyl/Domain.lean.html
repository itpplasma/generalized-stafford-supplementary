9 # The Weyl algebra is a domain
10 
11 For every field `k` (of any characteristic) and every `n`, the Weyl algebra
12 `A_n(k)` is a domain. The Bernstein symbol identifies the associated graded
13 ring of the Bernstein filtration with the polynomial ring `k[x, ∂]`, which is
14 a domain with `1` of degree `0`; `RingFiltration.GradedSymbol.isDomain` lifts
15 this to `A_n(k)`.
16 -/
17 
18 namespace AlgebraicAnalysis.Weyl

20 variable (k : Type*) [Field k] (n : ℕ)

22 /-- **The Weyl algebra has no zero divisors**: the product of two nonzero
23 elements of `A_n(k)` is nonzero. -/
24 theorem noZeroDivisors : NoZeroDivisors (WeylAlg k n) :=
25   (BernsteinSymbol.symbol k n).noZeroDivisors
26 
27 /-- **The Weyl algebra `A_n(k)` is a domain**, for every field `k`. -/
28 instance isDomain : IsDomain (WeylAlg k n) :=
29   (BernsteinSymbol.symbol k n).isDomain (MvPolynomial.isWeightedHomogeneous_one k _)
