8 # Symbol ring of the Weyl algebra
9 
10 The commutative symbol ring of `A_n(k)`: polynomials in the `2n` phase
11 variables `x_i` (fibre weight `0`) and `∂_i` (fibre weight `1`).
12 
13 Provenance: `SymbolRing` and `fibreWeight` are copied from
14 `Stafford38/Characteristic/Polynomial.lean` of `itpplasma/stafford38-formal` at
15 commit `ac20b730fb368786e840320ebf6e62d19bbe29f3` (Apache-2.0). The
16 Poisson bracket and characteristic-support definitions of that file are not
17 part of this extraction.
18 -/

22 /-- Coordinate ring of the affine phase space. -/
23 abbrev SymbolRing (k : Type*) [CommRing k] (n : ℕ) :=
24   MvPolynomial (PhaseVar n) k
25 
26 /-- Fibre degree: base variables have weight zero and covariables weight one. -/
27 def fibreWeight {n : ℕ} : PhaseVar n → ℕ
28   | Sum.inl _ => 0
29   | Sum.inr _ => 1
30 
31 end AlgebraicAnalysis.Weyl
