Transposition of the presented Weyl algebra

This file constructs the scalar-preserving Weyl transposition as an algebra equivalence with the opposite algebra. Coordinates are fixed and momenta change sign. The construction uses only the checked universal property of the quotient presentation.

The scalar-preserving anti-homomorphism xi​↦xi​, pi​↦−pi​, represented as an algebra homomorphism to the opposite algebra.

113/-- The scalar-preserving anti-homomorphism `x_i ↦ x_i`, `p_i ↦ -p_i`,
114represented as an algebra homomorphism to the opposite algebra. -/
115def transpositionHom (n : Nat) :
116 PresentedWeyl k n →ₐ[k] (PresentedWeyl k n)ᵐᵒᵖ :=
117 freeWeylLift (Matrix.J (Fin n) k) (transposedGenerator k n)
118 (transposedGenerator_commutator k n)

Applying transposition once on each side of the opposite equivalence is the identity.

132/-- Applying transposition once on each side of the opposite equivalence is
133the identity. -/
134theorem opComm_transpositionHom_comp_transpositionHom (n : Nat) :
135 (AlgHom.opComm (transpositionHom k n)).comp (transpositionHom k n) =
136 AlgHom.id k (PresentedWeyl k n) := by
137 apply freeWeyl_algHom_ext
138 intro i
139 cases i with
140 | inl i =>
141 simp only [AlgHom.comp_apply, AlgHom.id_apply]
142 rw [show freeWeylGenerator (Matrix.J (Fin n) k) (.inl i) =
143 coordinate k n i from rfl, transpositionHom_coordinate]
144 change unop (transpositionHom k n (coordinate k n i)) = coordinate k n i
145 rw [transpositionHom_coordinate]
146 exact unop_op _
147 | inr i =>
148 simp only [AlgHom.comp_apply, AlgHom.id_apply]
149 rw [show freeWeylGenerator (Matrix.J (Fin n) k) (.inr i) =
150 momentum k n i from rfl, transpositionHom_momentum]
151 change unop (transpositionHom k n (-momentum k n i)) = momentum k n i
152 rw [map_neg, transpositionHom_momentum]
153 simp
154
155theorem transpositionHom_comp_opComm_transpositionHom (n : Nat) :
156 (transpositionHom k n).comp (AlgHom.opComm (transpositionHom k n)) =
157 AlgHom.id k (PresentedWeyl k n)ᵐᵒᵖ := by
158 apply AlgHom.ext
159 intro a
160 rcases a with ⟨a⟩
161 apply unop_injective
162 have h := congrArg (fun f : PresentedWeyl k n →ₐ[k] PresentedWeyl k n => f a)
163 (opComm_transpositionHom_comp_transpositionHom k n)
164 exact h

Transposition is a scalar-preserving equivalence with the opposite Weyl algebra.

166/-- Transposition is a scalar-preserving equivalence with the opposite Weyl
167algebra. -/
168def transpositionEquiv (n : Nat) :
169 PresentedWeyl k n ≃ₐ[k] (PresentedWeyl k n)ᵐᵒᵖ :=
170 AlgEquiv.ofAlgHom (transpositionHom k n) (AlgHom.opComm (transpositionHom k n))
171 (transpositionHom_comp_opComm_transpositionHom k n)
172 (opComm_transpositionHom_comp_transpositionHom k n)

193/-- The usual unbundled anti-automorphism underlying `transpositionEquiv`. -/
194def transpose (n : Nat) (a : PresentedWeyl k n) : PresentedWeyl k n :=

A right PresentedWeyl-module becomes a left module by restriction of scalars along transposition.

224/-- A right `PresentedWeyl`-module becomes a left module by restriction of

226def transposedLeftModule (n : Nat) (M : Type v) [AddCommMonoid M]
