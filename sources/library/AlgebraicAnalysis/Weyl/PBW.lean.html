Ordered PBW basis from Ore normal forms

The checked iterated Ore normal form gives a scalar-linear equivalence with the commutative polynomial symbol space. Transport across the independently proved presented/iterated algebra equivalence yields a free-module basis indexed by commutative monomials, without using monomial independence in the quotient presentation. Recursive all-degree formulas identify those basis vectors with ordered products of the named Weyl generators.
