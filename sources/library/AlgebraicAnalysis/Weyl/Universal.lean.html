The algebra map from the free algebra sending each generator to a chosen element.

25/-- The algebra map from the free algebra sending each generator to a chosen element. -/
26def freeWeylGeneratorMap (z : ι → A) : FreeAlgebra k ι →ₐ[k] A :=
27 FreeAlgebra.lift k z
28
29theorem freeWeylGeneratorMap_respects (omega : Matrix ι ι k) (z : ι → A)
30 (hz : ∀ i j, AlgebraicAnalysis.ringCommutator (z i) (z j) = algebraMap k A (omega i j)) :
31 ∀ ⦃a b : FreeAlgebra k ι⦄,
32 freeWeylRelation omega a b →
33 freeWeylGeneratorMap z a = freeWeylGeneratorMap z b
34 | _, _, ⟨i, j, rfl, rfl⟩ => by
35 simp only [freeWeylGeneratorMap, map_sub, map_mul,
36 FreeAlgebra.lift_ι_apply, AlgHom.commutes]
37 exact hz i j

The algebra map induced by a family with the prescribed commutators.

39/-- The algebra map induced by a family with the prescribed commutators. -/
40def freeWeylLift (omega : Matrix ι ι k) (z : ι → A)
41 (hz : ∀ i j, AlgebraicAnalysis.ringCommutator (z i) (z j) = algebraMap k A (omega i j)) :
42 FreeWeyl k ι omega →ₐ[k] A :=
43 RingQuot.liftAlgHom k
44 ⟨freeWeylGeneratorMap z, freeWeylGeneratorMap_respects omega z hz⟩
45
46@[simp] theorem freeWeylLift_generator (omega : Matrix ι ι k) (z : ι → A)
47 (hz : ∀ i j, AlgebraicAnalysis.ringCommutator (z i) (z j) = algebraMap k A (omega i j)) (i : ι) :
48 freeWeylLift omega z hz (freeWeylGenerator omega i) = z i := by
49 simp [freeWeylLift, freeWeylGenerator, freeWeylGeneratorMap,
50 RingQuot.liftAlgHom_mkAlgHom_apply]

Algebra maps out of the presented Weyl algebra are determined by the canonical generators.

52/-- Algebra maps out of the presented Weyl algebra are determined by the
53canonical generators. -/
54theorem freeWeyl_algHom_ext (omega : Matrix ι ι k)
55 (f g : FreeWeyl k ι omega →ₐ[k] A)
56 (h : ∀ i, f (freeWeylGenerator omega i) =
57 g (freeWeylGenerator omega i)) : f = g := by
58 apply RingQuot.ringQuot_ext' k
59 apply FreeAlgebra.hom_ext
60 funext i
61 exact h i
