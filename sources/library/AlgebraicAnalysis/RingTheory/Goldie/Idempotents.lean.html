Idempotents of the quotient ring with common denominators

Let R be a simple right Noetherian ring with classical right quotient ring Q, and let s=lengthQ​(QQ​)=goldieRankRR. Since Q is semisimple, QQ​ is a direct sum of s simple right ideals, and the decomposition 1=E1​+⋯+Es​ gives complete orthogonal idempotents with Ei​Q simple. Clearing denominators in R gives the device used for upper bounds on the number of generators of a module:

  • a regular c∈R with fi​=Ei​c∈R for all i;
  • regular ti​∈R with ri​=c−1Ei​ti​∈R, stated without inverses as cri​=Ei​ti​ in Q.

Then fi​rj​=0 for i=j, and ei​=fi​ri​=Ei​ti​=0 (Goldie.exists_idempotent_denominators).

As elsewhere, Q=(Q°)op with Q° = OppositeQuotientRing R; the right ideal Ei​Q of Q is the left ideal Q°⋅unopEi​ of Q°, and its simplicity is stated for that left ideal.

References

J. C. McConnell and J. C. Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2.1.3 and 2.3.6; T. Y. Lam, A First Course in Noncommutative Rings, §3 and §21 (idempotents attached to a direct sum decomposition). Nothing here is new.

9# Idempotents of the quotient ring with common denominators
10
11Let `R` be a simple right Noetherian ring with classical right quotient ring
12`Q`, and let `s = length_Q(Q_Q) = goldieRank R R`. Since `Q` is semisimple,
13`Q_Q` is a direct sum of `s` simple right ideals, and the decomposition
14`1 = E₁ + ⋯ + E_s` gives complete orthogonal idempotents with `Eᵢ Q` simple.
15Clearing denominators in `R` gives the device used for upper bounds on the
16number of generators of a module:
17
18* a regular `c ∈ R` with `fᵢ = Eᵢ c ∈ R` for all `i`;
19* regular `tᵢ ∈ R` with `rᵢ = c⁻¹ Eᵢ tᵢ ∈ R`, stated without inverses as
20 `c rᵢ = Eᵢ tᵢ` in `Q`.
21
22Then `fᵢ rⱼ = 0` for `i ≠ j`, and `eᵢ = fᵢ rᵢ = Eᵢ tᵢ ≠ 0`
23(`Goldie.exists_idempotent_denominators`).
24
25As elsewhere, `Q = (Q°)ᵐᵒᵖ` with `Q° = OppositeQuotientRing R`; the right ideal
26`Eᵢ Q` of `Q` is the left ideal `Q° · unop Eᵢ` of `Q°`, and its simplicity is
27stated for that left ideal.

63 let eq := Finite.equivFin s

66 have hVsup : iSup V = ⊤ := by

Idempotents with common denominators (the device of McConnell–Robson 2.3.6 used for generator bounds). Let R be simple and right Noetherian with classical right quotient ring Q and ι:R→Q. There are n=rk(RR​)=lengthQ​(QQ​) complete orthogonal idempotents E1​,…,En​ of Q with each Ei​Q simple, a regular c, regular ti​ and elements fi​,ri​ of R with

  • Ei​c=fi​ and cri​=Ei​ti​ in Q (so ri​=c−1Ei​ti​);
  • fi​rj​=0 for i=j;
  • fi​ri​=Ei​ti​ in Q, and fi​ri​=0.
90/-- **Idempotents with common denominators** (the device of McConnell–Robson 2.3.6
91used for generator bounds). Let `R` be simple and right Noetherian with
92classical right quotient ring `Q` and `ι : R → Q`. There are `n = rk(R_R) =
93length_Q(Q_Q)` complete orthogonal idempotents `E₁, …, E_n` of `Q` with each
94`Eᵢ Q` simple, a regular `c`, regular `tᵢ` and elements `fᵢ, rᵢ` of `R` with
95
96* `Eᵢ c = fᵢ` and `c rᵢ = Eᵢ tᵢ` in `Q` (so `rᵢ = c⁻¹ Eᵢ tᵢ`);
97* `fᵢ rⱼ = 0` for `i ≠ j`;
98* `fᵢ rᵢ = Eᵢ tᵢ` in `Q`, and `fᵢ rᵢ ≠ 0`. -/
99theorem exists_idempotent_denominators [IsSimpleRing R] [IsNoetherian Rᵐᵒᵖ R] :
100 ∃ (n : ℕ) (E : Fin n → QuotientRing R) (c : R) (t f r : Fin n → R),
101 (n : ℕ∞) = goldieRank R R ∧
102 CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents E ∧
103 (∀ i, IsSimpleModule (OppositeQuotientRing R)
104 (Submodule.span (OppositeQuotientRing R) {unop (E i)})) ∧
105 c ∈ R⁰ ∧ (∀ i, t i ∈ R⁰) ∧
106 (∀ i, E i * toQuotientRing R c = toQuotientRing R (f i)) ∧
107 (∀ i, toQuotientRing R c * toQuotientRing R (r i) = E i * toQuotientRing R (t i)) ∧
108 (∀ i j, i ≠ j → f i * r j = 0) ∧
109 (∀ i, toQuotientRing R (f i * r i) = E i * toQuotientRing R (t i)) ∧
110 (∀ i, f i * r i ≠ 0) := by
111 classical
112 let ι := toQuotientRing R
113 obtain ⟨n, e, hn, he, hsimple⟩ := exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_oppositeQuotientRing
114 (R := R)
115 let E : Fin n → QuotientRing R := fun i => op (e i)
116 have hE : CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents E := by
117 refine ⟨⟨fun i => ?_, fun i j hij => ?_⟩, ?_⟩
118 · change op (e i) * op (e i) = op (e i)
119 rw [← op_mul, he.idem i]
120 · change op (e i) * op (e j) = 0
121 rw [← op_mul, he.ortho (Ne.symm hij), op_zero]
122 · change ∑ i, opAddEquiv (e i) = 1
123 rw [← map_sum, he.complete]
124 rfl
125 have hE0 : ∀ i, E i ≠ 0 := by
126 intro i h0
127 have h2 : e i = 0 := op_injective h0
128 have h := (hsimple i).nontrivial
129 rw [h2] at h
130 exact (Submodule.nontrivial_iff_ne_bot.mp h) (by simp)
131 -- the right ideal of denominators clearing `E i`
132 let J : Fin n → Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R := fun i =>
133 { carrier := {x | ∃ a, E i * ι x = ι a}
134 add_mem' := by
135 rintro x y ⟨a, ha⟩ ⟨b, hb⟩
136 exact ⟨a + b, by rw [map_add, mul_add, ha, hb, map_add]⟩
137 zero_mem' := ⟨0, by rw [map_zero, mul_zero]⟩
138 smul_mem' := by
139 rintro c x ⟨a, ha⟩
140 refine ⟨a * unop c, ?_⟩
141 change E i * ι (x * unop c) = ι (a * unop c)
142 rw [map_mul, ← mul_assoc, ha, map_mul] }
143 obtain ⟨c, hc, hcJ⟩ := exists_mem_nonZeroDivisors_finset Finset.univ J fun i _ => by
144 obtain ⟨a, s, hs, -, hsa⟩ := rightOre_clear (S := R⁰) (E i)
145 exact ⟨s, ⟨a, hsa⟩, hs⟩
146 choose f hf using fun i => hcJ i (Finset.mem_univ i)
147 have hcu := isUnit_toQuotientRing hc
148 have hrt : ∀ i, ∃ r t : R, t ∈ R⁰ ∧ ι c * ι r = E i * ι t := by
149 intro i
150 obtain ⟨a, t, ht, -, hta⟩ := rightOre_clear (S := R⁰) (↑hcu.unit⁻¹ * E i)
151 refine ⟨a, t, ht, ?_⟩
152 rw [← hta, ← mul_assoc, ← mul_assoc]
153 change ↑hcu.unit * ↑hcu.unit⁻¹ * E i * ι t = E i * ι t
154 rw [Units.mul_inv, one_mul]
155 choose r t ht hrt using hrt
156 have hfr : ∀ i j, ι (f i * r j) = E i * E j * ι (t j) := by
157 intro i j
158 rw [map_mul, ← hf i, mul_assoc, hrt j, mul_assoc]
159 refine ⟨n, E, c, t, f, r, hn.trans (goldieRank_self R).symm, hE, hsimple, hc, ht, hf, hrt,
160 fun i j hij => ?_, fun i => ?_, fun i h0 => ?_⟩
161 · apply toQuotientRing_injective
162 change ι (f i * r j) = ι 0
163 rw [hfr, hE.ortho hij, zero_mul, map_zero]
164 · rw [hfr, (hE.idem i).eq]
165 · have h1 : E i * ι (t i) = 0 := by
166 rw [← (hE.idem i).eq, ← hfr]
167 rw [h0, map_zero]
168 exact hE0 i ((isUnit_toQuotientRing (ht i)).mul_left_eq_zero.mp h1)
