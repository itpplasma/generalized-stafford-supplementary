Goldie rank of modules over a simple right Noetherian ring

Let R be a ring whose regular elements form a right Ore set, with classical right quotient ring Q (Goldie.QuotientRing R). For a right R-module M the module of fractions MQ​ consists of the fractions ms−1 with s regular; it is a right Q-module. In Lean, right R-modules are Rop-modules, MQ​ is Mathlib's Ore localization of M at the opposite copy of R0 (Goldie.FractionModule R M), and it is a left module over Q° = Goldie.OppositeQuotientRing R, i.e. a right Q-module.

  • Goldie.goldieTorsionPart R M: the Goldie-torsion part t(M), the span of the elements killed by a regular element. It is the kernel of M→MQ​ (Goldie.ker_toFractionModule), so these elements already form a submodule (Goldie.mem_goldieTorsionPart_iff).
  • Goldie.goldieRank R M = length_Q(M_Q) (in N∞). It is additive on short exact sequences (Goldie.goldieRank_eq_add_of_exact), since localization is exact; it is monotone under injective maps and decreases under quotients; goldieRankR(Finm→Rop)=m∗goldieRankRR; and it vanishes exactly on Goldie-torsion modules (Goldie.goldieRank_eq_zero_iff).

Now assume R is simple and right Noetherian, so that Q is simple Artinian (Goldie's theorem, AlgebraicAnalysis.RingTheory.Goldie.QuotientRing).

  • Goldie.goldieRank_ne_top: a finitely generated M has finite Goldie rank, and Goldie.goldieRank_self = length_Q(Q_Q) is finite and positive.
  • Uniform dimension. Goldie.uniformDimGE_quotient_iff, Goldie.uniformDimLE_quotient_iff: the uniform dimension of M/t(M) is the Goldie rank of M; Goldie.uniformDimGE_self_iff, Goldie.uniformDimLE_self_iff: the uniform dimension of RR​ is goldieRankRR=lengthQ​(QQ​).
  • Goldie.goldieRank_le_card_mul: a module generated by m elements has Goldie rank at most m∗goldieRankRR; in uniform-dimension form Goldie.le_mul_of_uniformDim.

Proof of the uniform dimension bridge

The map M/t(M)→MQ​ is injective, and (ms−1)s=m lies in its image for every fraction. Hence M/t(M) and MQ​ contain direct sums of the same numbers of nonzero submodules (OreModule.uniformDimGE_iff_of_injective, by saturating submodules). As Q is semisimple, so is MQ​, and its uniform dimension is its length (Module.uniformDimGE_iff_le_length).

References

J. C. McConnell and J. C. Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2.1.16–2.1.17, 2.2.10–2.2.13 and 2.3.6; K. R. Goodearl and R. B. Warfield, An Introduction to Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2nd ed., Chapters 5, 6 and 10. Nothing here is new.

10# Goldie rank of modules over a simple right Noetherian ring

12Let `R` be a ring whose regular elements form a right Ore set, with classical
13right quotient ring `Q` (`Goldie.QuotientRing R`). For a right `R`-module `M`
14the module of fractions `M_Q` consists of the fractions `m s⁻¹` with `s`
15regular; it is a right `Q`-module. In Lean, right `R`-modules are
16`Rᵐᵒᵖ`-modules, `M_Q` is Mathlib's Ore localization of `M` at the opposite copy
17of `R⁰` (`Goldie.FractionModule R M`), and it is a left module over
18`Q° = Goldie.OppositeQuotientRing R`, i.e. a right `Q`-module.
19
20* `Goldie.goldieTorsionPart R M`: the Goldie-torsion part `t(M)`, the span of
21 the elements killed by a regular element. It is the kernel of `M → M_Q`
22 (`Goldie.ker_toFractionModule`), so these elements already form a submodule
23 (`Goldie.mem_goldieTorsionPart_iff`).
24* `Goldie.goldieRank R M = length_Q(M_Q)` (in `ℕ∞`). It is additive on short
25 exact sequences (`Goldie.goldieRank_eq_add_of_exact`), since localization is
26 exact; it is monotone under injective maps and decreases under quotients;
27 `goldieRank R (Fin m → Rᵐᵒᵖ) = m * goldieRank R R`; and it vanishes exactly on
28 Goldie-torsion modules (`Goldie.goldieRank_eq_zero_iff`).
29
30Now assume `R` is simple and right Noetherian, so that `Q` is simple Artinian
31(Goldie's theorem, `AlgebraicAnalysis.RingTheory.Goldie.QuotientRing`).
32
33* `Goldie.goldieRank_ne_top`: a finitely generated `M` has finite Goldie rank,
34 and `Goldie.goldieRank_self = length_Q(Q_Q)` is finite and positive.
35* **Uniform dimension.** `Goldie.uniformDimGE_quotient_iff`,
36 `Goldie.uniformDimLE_quotient_iff`: the uniform dimension of `M/t(M)` is the
37 Goldie rank of `M`; `Goldie.uniformDimGE_self_iff`,
38 `Goldie.uniformDimLE_self_iff`: the uniform dimension of `R_R` is
39 `goldieRank R R = length_Q(Q_Q)`.
40* `Goldie.goldieRank_le_card_mul`: a module generated by `m` elements has
41 Goldie rank at most `m * goldieRank R R`; in uniform-dimension form
42 `Goldie.le_mul_of_uniformDim`.

44## Proof of the uniform dimension bridge
45
46The map `M/t(M) → M_Q` is injective, and `(m s⁻¹) s = m` lies in its image for
47every fraction. Hence `M/t(M)` and `M_Q` contain direct sums of the same numbers
48of nonzero submodules (`OreModule.uniformDimGE_iff_of_injective`, by
49saturating submodules). As `Q` is semisimple, so is `M_Q`, and its uniform
50dimension is its length (`Module.uniformDimGE_iff_le_length`).

164/-- **Additivity of Goldie rank.** For a short exact sequence
165`0 → M → N → P → 0` of right `R`-modules, `rk N = rk M + rk P`; localization is
166exact and length is additive. -/

169 goldieRank R N = goldieRank R M + goldieRank R P :=
170 Module.length_eq_add_of_exact _ _ (localizationMap_injective hf) (localizationMap_surjective hg)

331/-- **Generators bound the normalized rank from below** (`⌈ρ(M)⌉ ≤ μ(M)`). If `M`
332is generated by a set of at most `m` elements, `M/t(M)` contains a direct sum of
333`ℓ` nonzero submodules and `R_R` contains no direct sum of `s + 1` nonzero right

335theorem le_mul_of_uniformDim {m ℓ s : ℕ} (g : Finset M) (hg : g.card ≤ m)
336 (hspan : Submodule.span Rᵐᵒᵖ (g : Set M) = ⊤)
337 (hℓ : UniformDimGE Rᵐᵒᵖ (M ⧸ goldieTorsionPart R M) ℓ) (hs : UniformDimLE Rᵐᵒᵖ R s) :
338 ℓ ≤ m * s := by
339 have h1 := uniformDimGE_quotient_iff.mp hℓ
340 have h2 := goldieRank_le_card_mul g hspan
341 have h3 := uniformDimLE_self_iff.mp hs
342 have : (ℓ : ℕ∞) ≤ (m * s : ℕ) := by
343 calc (ℓ : ℕ∞) ≤ g.card * goldieRank R R := h1.trans h2
344 _ ≤ m * s := by gcongr
345 _ = (m * s : ℕ) := (Nat.cast_mul m s).symm
