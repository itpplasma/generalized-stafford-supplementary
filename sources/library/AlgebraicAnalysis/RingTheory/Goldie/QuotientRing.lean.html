Goldie's theorem: the quotient ring of a simple right Noetherian ring

Let R be a simple right Noetherian ring. By Goldie.exists_right_ore its regular elements form a right Ore set, so R has a classical right quotient ring Q, whose elements are right fractions rs−1 with s regular. Goldie's theorem says that Q is a simple Artinian ring; by the Wedderburn–Artin theorem it is a matrix ring Mn​(D) over a division ring D.

Mathlib's OreLocalization builds left fractions s−1r. As in AlgebraicAnalysis.OreRightLocalization.RightOreLocalization, we form the Ore localization Q° of the opposite ring Rop at the opposite copy of R0 (Goldie.OppositeQuotientRing R) and set Q=(Q°)op (Goldie.QuotientRing R, definitionally RightOreLocalization R R⁰). Left ideals of Q° are right ideals of Q; simplicity and semisimplicity pass between a ring and its opposite (Mathlib instances).

The Ore set structure is data, so the results below take an instance [OreLocalization.OreSet (oppositeSubmonoid R⁰)]; Goldie.nonempty_oreSet_oppositeSubmonoid shows that one exists. (Different choices give isomorphic rings by the universal property; this is not used.)

Main results

  • Goldie.isSimpleRing_quotientRing: Q is a simple ring;
  • Goldie.isSemisimpleRing_quotientRing, Goldie.isArtinianRing_quotientRing, Goldie.isArtinianRing_oppositeQuotientRing: Q is semisimple, and left and right Artinian;
  • Goldie.exists_ringEquiv_matrix_divisionRing: Q≃Mn​(D) for some n=0 and division ring D;
  • Goldie.length_oppositeQuotientRing_ne_top, Goldie.length_oppositeQuotientRing_pos: Q has finite positive length as a right module over itself;
  • Goldie.toQuotientRing: the canonical ring map R→Q, injective and sending regular elements to units.

Proof

Write ι:R→Q for the canonical map; every q∈Q has the form q=ι(r)ι(t)−1 with t regular, ι is injective, and ι(t) is a unit for regular t.

*Every essential right ideal E of Q is Q.* The right ideal J=ι−1(E) of R is essential: for y=0, ι(y)Q meets E in some ι(y)q=0; writing q=ι(r)ι(t)−1 gives ι(yr)=ι(y)qι(t)∈E, and yr=0. By Goldie (exists_mem_nonZeroDivisors_of_isEssential) J contains a regular s, and ι(s)∈E is a unit.

*Q is semisimple.* For a right ideal L of Q choose, by Zorn's lemma, a right ideal L′ maximal with L∩L′=0. Then L+L′ is essential: if N∩(L+L′)=0, the modular law gives L∩(L′+N)=0, so N≤L′ by maximality and N=0. Hence L+L′=Q and L′ is a complement of L.

*Q is simple.* A nonzero two-sided ideal T of Q contains some q=ι(r)ι(t)−1=0, hence ι(r)=qι(t)=0; then ι−1(T) is a nonzero two-sided ideal of the simple ring R, so 1∈T.

A simple semisimple ring is Artinian, and Wedderburn–Artin applies (IsSimpleRing.exists_ringEquiv_matrix_divisionRing).

References

A. W. Goldie, The structure of prime rings under ascending chain conditions, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3) 8 (1958) 589–608; Semi-prime rings with maximum condition, ibid. 10 (1960) 201–220; J. C. McConnell and J. C. Robson, Noncommutative Noetherian Rings, 2.3.6 and 2.3.10. Nothing here is new.

9# Goldie's theorem: the quotient ring of a simple right Noetherian ring
10
11Let `R` be a simple right Noetherian ring. By `Goldie.exists_right_ore` its
12regular elements form a right Ore set, so `R` has a classical right quotient
13ring `Q`, whose elements are right fractions `r s⁻¹` with `s` regular. Goldie's
14theorem says that `Q` is a simple Artinian ring; by the Wedderburn–Artin
15theorem it is a matrix ring `Mₙ(D)` over a division ring `D`.
16
17Mathlib's `OreLocalization` builds left fractions `s⁻¹ r`. As in
18`AlgebraicAnalysis.OreRightLocalization.RightOreLocalization`, we form the Ore
19localization `Q°` of the opposite ring `Rᵐᵒᵖ` at the opposite copy of `R⁰`
20(`Goldie.OppositeQuotientRing R`) and set `Q = (Q°)ᵐᵒᵖ`
21(`Goldie.QuotientRing R`, definitionally `RightOreLocalization R R⁰`). Left
22ideals of `Q°` are right ideals of `Q`; simplicity and semisimplicity pass
23between a ring and its opposite (Mathlib instances).
24
25The Ore set structure is data, so the results below take an instance
26`[OreLocalization.OreSet (oppositeSubmonoid R⁰)]`;
27`Goldie.nonempty_oreSet_oppositeSubmonoid` shows that one exists. (Different
28choices give isomorphic rings by the universal property; this is not used.)

31
32* `Goldie.isSimpleRing_quotientRing`: `Q` is a simple ring;
33* `Goldie.isSemisimpleRing_quotientRing`, `Goldie.isArtinianRing_quotientRing`,

44## Proof
45
46Write `ι : R → Q` for the canonical map; every `q ∈ Q` has the form
47`q = ι(r) ι(t)⁻¹` with `t` regular, `ι` is injective, and `ι(t)` is a unit for
48regular `t`.
49
50*Every essential right ideal `E` of `Q` is `Q`.* The right ideal
51`J = ι⁻¹(E)` of `R` is essential: for `y ≠ 0`, `ι(y) Q` meets `E` in some
52`ι(y) q ≠ 0`; writing `q = ι(r) ι(t)⁻¹` gives `ι(y r) = ι(y) q ι(t) ∈ E`, and
53`y r ≠ 0`. By Goldie (`exists_mem_nonZeroDivisors_of_isEssential`) `J`
54contains a regular `s`, and `ι(s) ∈ E` is a unit.
55
56*`Q` is semisimple.* For a right ideal `L` of `Q` choose, by Zorn's lemma, a
57right ideal `L'` maximal with `L ∩ L' = 0`. Then `L + L'` is essential: if
58`N ∩ (L + L') = 0`, the modular law gives `L ∩ (L' + N) = 0`, so `N ≤ L'` by
59maximality and `N = 0`. Hence `L + L' = Q` and `L'` is a complement of `L`.
60
61*`Q` is simple.* A nonzero two-sided ideal `T` of `Q` contains some
62`q = ι(r) ι(t)⁻¹ ≠ 0`, hence `ι(r) = q ι(t) ≠ 0`; then `ι⁻¹(T)` is a nonzero
63two-sided ideal of the simple ring `R`, so `1 ∈ T`.
64
65A simple semisimple ring is Artinian, and Wedderburn–Artin applies
66(`IsSimpleRing.exists_ringEquiv_matrix_divisionRing`).

159theorem eq_top_of_forall_inf_eq_bot (E : Submodule (OppositeQuotientRing R)
160 (OppositeQuotientRing R)) (hE : ∀ N, N ⊓ E = ⊥ → N = ⊥) : E = ⊤ := by
161 let ι := numeratorRingHom (R := Rᵐᵒᵖ) (S := oppositeSubmonoid R⁰)
162 let J : Submodule Rᵐᵒᵖ R :=
163 { carrier := {r | ι (op r) ∈ E}
164 add_mem' := fun {x y} hx hy => by
165 change ι (op (x + y)) ∈ E
166 rw [op_add, map_add]
167 exact E.add_mem hx hy
168 zero_mem' := by
169 change ι (op 0) ∈ E
170 rw [op_zero, map_zero]
171 exact E.zero_mem
172 smul_mem' := fun c x hx => by
173 change ι (op (x * unop c)) ∈ E
174 rw [op_mul, op_unop, map_mul]
175 exact E.smul_mem (ι c) hx }
176 have hJ : IsEssential J := by
177 intro y hy
178 have hq0 : ι (op y) ≠ 0 := fun h =>
179 hy (op_injective (numeratorRingHom_injective (h.trans (map_zero ι).symm)))
180 have hspan : Submodule.span _ {ι (op y)} ⊓ E ≠ ⊥ := fun h =>
181 hq0 (Submodule.span_singleton_eq_bot.mp (hE _ h))
182 obtain ⟨z, ⟨hzspan, hzE⟩, hz0⟩ := Submodule.exists_mem_ne_zero_of_ne_bot hspan
183 obtain ⟨q, rfl⟩ := Submodule.mem_span_singleton.mp hzspan
184 obtain ⟨t, ht, r, hr⟩ := exists_numerator_mul_eq q
185 have key : ι (op (y * unop r)) = ι t * (q • ι (op y)) := by
186 rw [op_mul, op_unop, map_mul, ← hr, smul_eq_mul, mul_assoc]
187 refine ⟨unop r, fun h0 => hz0 ?_, ?_⟩
188 · have h1 : ι t * (q • ι (op y)) = 0 := by rw [← key, h0, op_zero, map_zero]
189 exact (isUnit_numeratorRingHom ht).mul_right_eq_zero.mp h1

191 rw [key]
