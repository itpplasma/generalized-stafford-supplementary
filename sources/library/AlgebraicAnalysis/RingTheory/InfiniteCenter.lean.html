8 # Infinite centre of an infinite-dimensional-or-not algebra over an infinite field
9 
10 Let `k` be an infinite field and `A` a nontrivial `k`-algebra. The structure map
11 `k → A` is injective (a ring map out of a field into a nontrivial ring) and its
12 image is central, because `k` acts through scalars. Hence the centre of `A`
13 contains an infinite set. This is the hypothesis `(Z)` of the filtered-ring
14 results: the centre is infinite.
15 
16 ## Main results
17 
18 * `AlgebraicAnalysis.infinite_subring_center`: `Infinite (Subring.center A)`.

23 /-- **Infinite centre.** A nontrivial algebra over an infinite field has an
24 infinite centre. -/
25 theorem infinite_subring_center (k A : Type*) [Field k] [Infinite k] [Ring A]
26     [Nontrivial A] [Algebra k A] : Infinite (Subring.center A) := by
27   have hinj : Function.Injective (algebraMap k A) := (algebraMap k A).injective
28   have hsub : Set.range (algebraMap k A) ⊆ (Subring.center A : Set A) := by
29     rintro _ ⟨c, rfl⟩
30     rw [SetLike.mem_coe, Subring.mem_center_iff]
31     intro g
32     exact (Algebra.commutes c g).symm
33   exact Set.infinite_coe_iff.mpr ((Set.infinite_range_of_injective hinj).mono hsub)
34 
35 end AlgebraicAnalysis
