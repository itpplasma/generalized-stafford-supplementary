Infinite centre of an infinite-dimensional-or-not algebra over an infinite field

Let k be an infinite field and A a nontrivial k-algebra. The structure map k→A is injective (a ring map out of a field into a nontrivial ring) and its image is central, because k acts through scalars. Hence the centre of A contains an infinite set. This is the hypothesis (Z) of the filtered-ring results: the centre is infinite.

Infinite centre. A nontrivial algebra over an infinite field has an infinite centre.

23/-- **Infinite centre.** A nontrivial algebra over an infinite field has an
24infinite centre. -/
25theorem infinite_subring_center (k A : Type*) [Field k] [Infinite k] [Ring A]
26 [Nontrivial A] [Algebra k A] : Infinite (Subring.center A) := by
27 have hinj : Function.Injective (algebraMap k A) := (algebraMap k A).injective
28 have hsub : Set.range (algebraMap k A) ⊆ (Subring.center A : Set A) := by
29 rintro _ ⟨c, rfl⟩
30 rw [SetLike.mem_coe, Subring.mem_center_iff]
31 intro g
32 exact (Algebra.commutes c g).symm
33 exact Set.infinite_coe_iff.mpr ((Set.infinite_range_of_injective hinj).mono hsub)
